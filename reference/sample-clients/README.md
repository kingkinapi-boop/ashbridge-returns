# Sample clients (all made up)

Ten made-up Ontario corporations, each with a year of bank and card transactions, what the client app would hand over at onboarding, and an answer key. They are the first version of the test world (decision 0008, Z8-8): loaded into QBO test companies, run through the Taxprep trial, and later used by the build's tests. The build's test-world cards extend these ten; they never invent a second set.

## Rules
- Everything is made up. Company and person names end in "(Test)"; inside bank descriptions a person's name carries the word TEST. Business numbers and SINs are generated to fail their check digit, so none can belong to anyone. Banks, card issuers, brokers and customers are made up; merchants may be common chains (fuel, coffee, telecom, software, groceries) because real statements always show them.
- Amounts in dollars and cents with two decimals, no thousands separators. Ontario HST 13%.
- Every account's balance rolls: opening plus the month's activity equals closing, every month, except where a client's planted fault says otherwise.
- Regenerate with `node reference/sample-clients/generate.mjs` (fixed seed, no packages). Check with `node reference/sample-clients/verify.mjs`.
- GIFI codes come only from CRA's GIFI guide (RC4088). A mapping that guide does not settle is marked "confirm" for the CPA.
- Taxprep computes tax. Answer keys hold inputs (GIFI lines, schedule inputs, additions by CCA class, adjusting entries) and the flags that must fire, not tax payable.

## Status (29 Sep 2026)
- Generated and verified: `node reference/sample-clients/verify.mjs` gives 236 passes and no failures; a second generation is byte-identical. About 8,800 rows across 23 accounts.
- **For the CPA to confirm** (marked "confirm" in the answer keys): GIFI codes for the card payable, brokerage cash, tax instalments, suspense, deposits received, current against long-term shareholder loans, the equipment loan, card fees, CRA penalties and income tax withheld; the quick-method rate used (8.8%); the 50% capital-gain inclusion; the class 10.1 cost limit; whether a T5 is needed for a corporate shareholder.
- **Left as flags for a person, not decided:** home office, the grant, research costs, the spouse's pay, personal spending, the SUV's personal use, the capital dividend election, GRIP and personal services business signs. The spouse's pay and the grant sit in a suspense account until then.
- **Choices to know:** layouts B, C and the card have no balance column, so month-end statement balances are in each `profile.md` and answer key. Events after year end (client 03's bonus on 28 Dec 2025, client 01's Q4 HST on 30 Jan 2026) are in onboarding and the answer key, not the twelve-month files. SINs appear only in answer keys. Payroll deductions and exchange rates are simulated test figures.

## What each client folder holds
| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `profile.md` | Who they are, the planted issues with amounts and dates, and what each check should find |
| `onboarding.json` | What the client app hands over: services, year end, owners and shares, related companies, staff and payroll totals, HST method and frequency, home office share, vehicle use, which personal-card items are business, prior-year closing balances, the client's own notes |
| `accounts/*.csv` | Twelve months per account in that bank's own export layout |
| `qbo/*.csv` | The same transactions in QBO's three-column import layout |
| `answer-key.json` | The right account for every transaction, the adjusting entries with reasons and sources, the trial balance by account and GIFI code, the T2 inputs, and the flags that must fire |

## Export layouts (made-up institutions, realistic shapes)
- **A: Lakeview Bank (Test), chequing.** Header row. `Date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `Description`, `Withdrawals`, `Deposits`, `Balance`.
- **B: Maplestone Bank (Test), chequing.** Two header lines (account type; masked account number), then `Transaction Date` (MM/DD/YYYY), `Description 1`, `Description 2`, `CAD$` or `USD$` (negative is money out).
- **C: Harbourline Credit Union (Test), chequing.** No header. `DD/MM/YYYY`, description, debit, credit.
- **Card: Aurora Card (Test).** Header row. `Transaction Date`, `Posting Date`, `Description`, `Amount` (positive is a charge, negative a payment or refund).
- **Brokerage: Crestview Investing (Test).** Monthly activity: `Date`, `Activity`, `Security`, `Quantity`, `Amount`, `Cash Balance`.
- **QBO import:** `Date` (DD/MM/YYYY), `Description`, `Amount` (negative is money out).
