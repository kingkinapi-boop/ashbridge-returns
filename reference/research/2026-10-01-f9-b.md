## 1. T2 review checklist for a small Ontario CCPC

Template shape [fact, financial-cents.com T2 checklist, opened]: ten sections: client and entity setup (legal name, BN, address, corporation type, prior filings); financial statements (GIFI entry, Schedule 1 book-to-tax, shareholder loan continuity); income (active, investment, intercorporate dividends, capital gains); deductions (CCA continuity by class, non-deductibles, reserves); small business deduction (ABI, business limit, grind for passive income and taxable capital, Schedule 23 associations); investment tax credits; related parties and shareholders (loans receivable and the one-year rule, Schedules 9 and 11); corporation-type items (RDTOH, GRIP Schedule 53); provincial (Schedule 5, rates); filing and sign-off (6-month filing, 2- or 3-month payment, instalment reconciliation, dual sign-off).
Proposed order for the CPA screen [inference], with what is checked:
1. Identity and year: BN, name, year end, short or long year, CCPC status, opening balances equal last year's closing, Auto-fill differences.
2. Statements tie: balance sheet balances, net income equals income statement, retained earnings roll (opening + net income - dividends = closing) [fact for CRA validity checks, prior file CRA9].
3. Schedule 1: every add-back and deduction has a source; book amortization back, CCA in; meals 50%; non-deductibles (penalties, personal items); reserves; income tax provision added back.
4. Capital: Schedule 8 continuity by class, additions and disposals tied to the ledger, Schedule 6 dispositions.
5. Losses: Schedule 4 balances equal Auto-fill and last year's file.
6. Rate: ABI vs investment income (Schedule 7), business limit, Schedule 23 association sharing, taxable capital grind.
7. Dividend accounts: GRIP, RDTOH (eligible and non-eligible), Part IV tax, CDA rolled from last year's closing.
8. Shareholders and related parties: Schedules 50, 9, 11; shareholder loan continuity; T4 and T5 totals match salary and dividends.
9. Provincial: Schedule 5 allocation, Ontario credits and rates.
10. Disclosures: T1135, Schedule 141 who prepared the statements.
11. Payment and filing: balance-due date, next-year instalments, signed and dated T183CORP before e-file, diagnostics cleared, review marks complete.
Practice-inspection evidence [fact, bccpa.ca 2023-24 findings, opened]: common T1 and T2 deficiencies were missed client filing deadlines, no file note on foreign income and assets, and no signed and dated T183 before e-filing; improper deductions and CCA errors without support can fail a firm's review. [fact, bccpa.ca search summary only, not itemised on the page text I read] checklists filled with bare Y or N and no explanation count against a firm. [inference] Each checklist item on our screen needs a one-line note or a source link, not a bare tick.

## 2. Owner-manager issues (issue: data that signals it)

Sources opened: connectcpa.ca shareholder loan page, mccayduff.com PSB page, financial-cents template. Rows marked [inference] rest on general practice and prior files; I opened no page that states them.
- Shareholder loans [fact, connectcpa]: a debit at year end in due-from-shareholder (1301) is included in the shareholder's income unless repaid within one year after the corporation's year end (15(2.6)); repay-and-reborrow ("series of loans") defeats it; below-market or nil interest creates an 80.4 benefit even when 15(2.6) applies; personal cards, undocumented draws and family payments are the usual sources. Needs written terms, a resolution, a loan ledger.
- Remuneration mix [inference]: T4 total vs payroll expense (9060) vs draws; dividends with no resolution or T5; dividends above retained earnings; no salary at all.
- Personal expenses [inference]: owner-named card statements, round amounts, large "other" (9270) or office lines, meals at 100% in 8523.
- Vehicles [inference]: vehicle expense (9281) with no logbook; motor vehicle additions (1742), class 10.1 cap; standby charge or operating benefit missing from the T4.
- Home office [inference]: rent or utilities paid to the owner or on a home address. No GIFI code for business use of home found on RC4088 (not found).
- CDA [inference]: capital gains disposed (Schedule 6), insurance proceeds; a capital dividend election needs a positive balance first; compare the balance with Auto-fill.
- RDTOH and dividend refunds [inference]: investment income (Schedule 7), T5 slips issued, eligible vs non-eligible dividends paid, Part IV tax on portfolio dividends received. Dividend refund rate not verified.
- Association [fact on Schedule 23 role, financial-cents]; signal [inference]: other corporations owned by owner, spouse or related persons, transactions between them on Schedules 9 and 11, business limit not shared.
- PSB [fact, McCay Duff]: all of: 10 percent or greater shareholder, no more than five full-time employees, and the worker would be an employee of the client without the corporation (the hard test); the services are not to an associated corporation. Ontario combined rate about 12.2 percent for an SBD CCPC vs 44.5 percent for a PSB; PSB expenses limited to salary, benefits, legal collection and what an employee could deduct. Signal [inference]: one client, hourly billing, client's equipment, no staff, a former employer.
- SIB [inference, no page opened]: rent or interest income with five or fewer employees loses the SBD.
- Instalments [prior file CRA21]: monthly if tax payable over $3,000 in either of the last two years; the test is code, not AI.

## 4. Taxprep T2 diagnostics

[fact, Taxprep help "About Diagnostics" 2025 and "Types of Diagnostics" 2025 v20, both opened]. Types: Mandatory modification (must change data so the return is not rejected); Missing information (fill the indicated cells); Error (review, correct if needed); Possible input error; Filing (verify the forms list); Review (overridden cells); Optimization; Tax planning possibilities; Exclusion (why bar codes cannot print); a filter tool. The Take Action tab shows unresolved items; each can be annotated Reviewed or Ignored, as first or second review, and moves to its own tab. Help's own example of something annotated rather than cleared: a filing reminder.
Blocks e-filing [fact, Taxprep "Correct an Error Detected by a Diagnostic" page plus search text of the EFILE page]: EFILE-tab diagnostics (generated only once the return is selected for EFILE); errors "that can cause the return to be rejected" must be corrected before the workchart shows the return eligible. The page also says to read and correct all diagnostics before filing, warnings included.
CRA-side rejects [fact, Taxprep T2 EFILE error codes page, opened]: 340-347 missing incorporation data, 355-399 tax year dates (387 already assessed), 501-513 file format, 534 attachments over 150 MB, 881 uncertified software.
Routinely ignorable [inference; no named practitioner found]: Optimization, Tax planning possibilities, filing reminders, Review items once the override is explained, and warnings restating a known situation (for example a short year).
Design [inference]: block = Mandatory modification, Missing information, EFILE-tab errors; must-explain = Error, Possible input error; may-ignore-with-note = the rest. "Ignored" is an annotation, so log who ignored what and why, first and second review.
