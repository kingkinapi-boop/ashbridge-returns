## CK-30 Shareholder loan not repaid in time (15(2), 15(2.6))
- Rule [fact, s.15(2), s.15(2.6), Folio S3-F1-C1 para 1.71]: a loan to a shareholder (or connected person) because of shareholding is income of the borrower for the year of the loan, unless repaid within one year after the end of the LENDER's tax year in which the loan was made, and the repayment is not part of a series of loans or other transactions and repayments.
- Test: deadline = lender year end + 1 year (not loan date + 1 year). Flag if loan balance at that deadline > 0 after applying repayments oldest-first (FIFO, Folio para 1.81, "unless the facts clearly indicate otherwise"). Also flag as "at risk" when the lender's year end has passed and balance is open: the 15(2) inclusion is claimed in the BORROWER's year of the loan; if the borrower's return anticipated 15(2.6), CK-32 benefit was included instead (see S3-F1-C2 paras 2.34-2.35: amend to swap if repayment fails).
- Exceptions to suppress the flag (record, do not hide): 15(2.3) ordinary lending business (bona fide repayment terms within reasonable time); 15(2.4) employee loans (non-specified employee, dwelling, shares, vehicle; made because of employment, with reasonable repayment terms); 15(2.2) non-resident to non-resident.
- Example: Lender year end 31 Dec 2025. Owner borrows 40,000 on 10 Jun 2025. Deadline 31 Dec 2026. Repaid 25,000 on 15 Mar 2026; 15,000 open on 31 Dec 2026. Flag 15,000 (income inclusion in the 2025 year of the borrower, then 20(1)(j) deduction when later repaid).
- Folio example paras 1.80-1.81: 50,000 borrowed 30 Jun Y1, lender year end 31 Dec; repayment 14 Jun Y2 is in time.

## CK-31 Series of loans and repayments (15(2.6))
- Rule [fact, Folio para 1.83-1.84]: if repayment is part of a series, the 15(2.6) exception does not apply and no 20(1)(j) deduction. Para 1.84: a repayment is generally viewed as part of a series where all or part of a loan is repaid BEFORE the lender's year end and an amount is re-borrowed. CRA gives no safe period of days; it is fact-based. Para 1.86: repayments made by applying dividends, salary or bonus owed to the borrower are not treated as a series, even if borrowing resumes.
- Test (code, conservative): for each repayment R dated within the lender-year-end-plus-one-year window of a loan: flag if any new advance A to the same borrower is dated within the same lender fiscal year as R, or within the same period as R after the lender's year end, and A.amount >= 50% of R (50% is OUR choice, amber it) AND R was not funded by a dividend or salary credit on the shareholder account. Records needed: advance date and amount, repayment date, source of repayment (cash, dividend credit, bonus credit, new loan).
- Example: lender year end 31 Dec. Loan 30,000 on 1 Mar 2025. Owner repays 30,000 on 20 Dec 2025 by cheque, re-borrows 28,000 on 8 Jan 2026. Flag: repay before year end then re-borrow. Same facts but repayment made by declaring a 30,000 dividend credited to the loan: no flag (para 1.86).
- Note [inference]: the text quoted in the tool summary placed the series wording inside 15(2.6) itself; Folio para 1.84 is the CRA view. RECHECK exact subsection numbering on laws-lois before citing in the product.

## CK-32 No interest on shareholder loan (80.4(2))
- Rule [fact, s.80.4(2), Folio S3-F1-C2 paras 2.2, 2.25]: if the shareholder loan is not included under 15(2), a deemed benefit = interest at the prescribed rate on the outstanding balance for the period, minus interest actually paid in the year or within 30 days after the year end. Rate is set quarterly from 90 day T-bill (para 2.27). Para 2.15: 80.4(2) does not apply to a loan already included in income under 15(2). 80.4(3) exceptions: arm's-length rate loans in a lending business.
- The fetched s.80.4 summary said "non-resident" for 80.4(2); the folio says shareholder or connected person generally. Trust the folio; RECHECK the Act.
- Test: for each loan balance with no interest line, benefit = sum over quarters(balance x prescribed rate for that quarter x days/365) - interest paid by (year end + 30 days). Flag if benefit > 0. Prescribed rate table: not fetched; load from CRA's prescribed interest rates page as data (data before code).
- Example (folio Example 2, para 2.25): 100,000 loan at 0.75%; prescribed 1% for two quarters and 1.25% for two quarters; interest paid 500 in year and 250 on 10 Jan next year. A = 1,125; B = 750; benefit 375.

## CK-33 Unpaid bonus or salary, 180 days (78(4))
- Rule [fact, quote]: remuneration "unpaid on the day that is 180 days after the end of the taxation year in which the expense was incurred" is deemed not incurred in that year and deducted in the year paid. Excludes reasonable vacation pay and salary deferral arrangements. (Distinct: 78(1) is the two-year rule for other non-arm's-length expenses, with an election.)
- Test: for each accrued payroll liability at year end, deadline = year end + 180 days (day count, so 31 Dec 2025 to 29 Jun 2026). Flag if unpaid on deadline, or if the filing date is before the deadline and amount still unpaid (warn). Needs: payment date from payroll or the books.
- Example: year end 31 Dec 2025, bonus accrued 20,000. Paid 15 Jul 2026 (after 29 Jun). Deduction moves to the FY2026 return; the 2025 return must add 20,000 back (Schedule 1).

## CK-34 Specified investment business (125(7))
- Rule [fact, s.125(7)]: a business whose principal purpose is income from property (interest, rent, royalties, dividends), unless the corporation employs MORE THAN five full-time employees throughout the year (or an associated corporation supplies substantial services). Income is not active business income, so no small business deduction.
- CRA view [inference; search snippet on Technical News 41, not opened]: "more than five" is met by five full-time employees plus one or more part-time employees. RECHECK by opening the page.
- "Full-time" has no number in the Act summary: not found; CRA practice hours not confirmed. Do not hard-code hours; flag for a person.
- Test: property income (rent, interest, dividends) is the principal source (more than 50% of gross revenue, our test, amber) AND count of full-time employees (T4 slips for people on payroll all year) <= 5 -> flag; show the T4 count.
- Example: rental corporation, gross revenue 120,000 rent, 2 full-time T4 employees. Flag. Same with 6 full-time employees: no flag.

## CK-35 Personal services business (125(7))
- Rule [fact, s.125(7) summary]: a corporation carries on a services business where the individual who performs the services (or a person related to them) is a specified shareholder of the corporation, and the individual would reasonably be regarded as an officer or employee of the client, unless the corporation has MORE THAN five full-time employees throughout the year or the amount is received from an associated corporation. Income is not active business income, no SBD. [inference] Other tax effects (no business expense deductions beyond salary, higher federal rate) are in s.18(1)(p) and 123.5, not opened.
- Test (signs, code): owner is a specified shareholder (10%+) AND one client supplies >= 90% of revenue (90% is OUR choice, amber) AND employees <= 5. Always a flag for a person, never a verdict.
- Example: a consultant's corporation bills 200,000 to one customer, owner 100% shareholder, no other staff, works on the client's site on its hours. Flag.

## CK-36 Passive income grind (125(5.1)) and Ontario
- Rule [fact]: the federal business limit (500,000) is reduced by the GREATER of (a) the taxable capital reduction and (b) BL/500,000 x 5 x (AAII - 50,000). Taxable capital reduction uses 0.225% x (C - 10,000,000) in the formula (the summary was garbled on the divisor: RECHECK). The AAII is measured for the tax years ENDING in the PRECEDING calendar year, of the corporation and all associated corporations combined (CRA SBD rules page). Active asset capital gains and income incident to an active business are excluded. At AAII 150,000 the limit is nil.
- Ontario [fact, Ontario SBD page]: the Ontario SBD is NOT reduced by passive income. It IS phased out by taxable capital employed in Canada over 10 million to nil at 50 million (15 million before tax years starting 7 Apr 2022). Ontario SBD rate 8.3%, leaving 3.2%.
- Test: AAII = sum over associated group of the preceding calendar year year ends; flag if AAII > 50,000 and show the federal limit.
- Example (CRA page): AAII 75,000 gives reduction 5 x 25,000 = 125,000, limit 375,000. Ontario limit stays 500,000 (before taxable capital).

## CK-37 Foreign reporting (T1135, T106, T1134)
- T1135 [fact, s.233.3 and T1135 Q&A]: file if the total COST amount (not FMV) of specified foreign property exceeds 100,000 at any time in the year; Part A (check boxes) if under 250,000 throughout, Part B (detail) if 250,000 or more at any time. Due with the income tax return, even if no return is required. Excludes property used in an active business, shares of a foreign affiliate, personal-use property, RRSP/TFSA.
- T106 [fact, s.233.1(4)]: file if reportable transactions with non-arm's length non-residents total more than 1,000,000 in the year (FMV of property or services). Due on the filing-due date.
- T1134 [fact, s.233.4(4), T1134 page]: a reporting entity (Canadian resident with a foreign affiliate) files for each foreign affiliate within 10 months after the year end. No dollar threshold. A foreign affiliate threshold (1% equity, 10% with related) is in s.95(4), not opened: RECHECK.
- Test: foreign accounts or property with a cost over 100,000 in the books; total non-resident related party transactions over 1,000,000; any investment in a foreign corporation (equity shares) -> flag.
- Example: corporation holds 130,000 (cost) of US shares in a brokerage account: T1135 Part A due with the T2. A parent paid 1,200,000 in management fees to its non-resident sister company: T106.

## CK-38 T4 and T5 implied (filing)
- T5 [fact, CRA T5 page]: corporations prepare T5 for eligible and other dividends (including most deemed dividends) to Canadian residents, but not where the total to one recipient for the year is less than 50. Capital dividends do not need a T5.
- T4 [fact for the rule; the page was a search summary]: remuneration is reported on the T4 for the year it is PAID, not the year it is earned; file a T4 only if remuneration is more than 500 (search snippet, RECHECK). So an accrued, unpaid bonus is NOT on a T4 until paid. The blueprint line "accrued bonuses need T4s" should read "bonuses paid in the year need T4s": suggest an amber to fix CK-38.
- Due [fact]: last day of February after the calendar year for both; penalty minimum 100 for few slips (page quoted: 1-5 slips 100 flat; larger tiers per page); T5 minimum 100, maximum 7,500.
- Test: dividends paid in calendar year to resident recipient >= 50 and no T5 on CRA data; salary or bonus paid in the calendar year over 500 and no T4. Needs CRA account data (T4/T5 filed).
- Example: 30,000 dividend paid to the owner in 2025, no T5 by 28 Feb 2026: flag.

## CK-41 Excess eligible dividend designation and capital dividend election
- GRIP [fact, s.89(1)]: GRIP = A - B; A includes prior year GRIP, general rate factor x adjusted taxable income, eligible dividends received, other adjustments; B takes off prior year eligible dividends paid (and excessive designations). Eligible dividend: a taxable dividend designated under 89(14).
- Part III.1 [fact, s.185.1]: tax on an excessive eligible dividend designation: 20% (185.1(1)(a)); a further 10% in a limited case (185.1(1)(b), via para (c) of the definition); election 185.1(2) to treat the excess as an ordinary dividend. CCPC, GRIP at end of the year the dividend is paid. RECHECK 185.1(1)(a) rate on the page.
- Test: eligible dividends designated in the year (from minute book, T5) > GRIP at the end of that year (T2 Schedule 53) -> flag.
- Capital dividend [fact, s.83(2), s.184]: election must be made at or before the first day any part of the dividend is paid (or the day it becomes payable if earlier), in prescribed form (T2054), and the dividend is capital only up to the capital dividend account just before that time. Excess: 3/5 (60%) tax under 184(2), with 184(3) election to recharacterise within 90 days of the assessment notice. Not opened: T2054 filing guide (the form page had no filing detail).
- Test: any dividend recorded as "capital" with no T2054 date on or before payment -> flag; also flag if capital dividend > CDA balance.
- Example: GRIP at year end 40,000. Corporation designates 55,000 of dividends as eligible. Excess 15,000; Part III.1 tax 20% x 15,000 = 3,000.
