## CK-10 Balance sheet balances
- Rule: total assets (GIFI 2599) = total liabilities (3499) + total shareholder equity (3620); and 3640 "Total liabilities and shareholder equity" equals 2599. [fact, S1 codes 3620 and 3640; S2 shows 2599, 3499, 3640 and 3849 as the must-report fields]
- Worked: 2599 = 148,200; 3499 = 61,000; 3620 = 87,200 (share capital 100 + retained 87,100). 61,000 + 87,200 = 148,200 = 3640. Pass.
- Edge: Schedule 100 lists accumulated amortization on its own lines (1681, 1743, 1775, 1788, 1919, 2009). Whether the export carries them as positive numbers to be subtracted or as negatives is not stated on the form [not found]; a tie that adds them all will be off by twice the amount. Rule for the check: derive the sign convention from the export once (the trial week) and test 2008 less 2009 against the sum of the asset lines, not just the grand total.
- Edge: tie the three totals (2599, 3499 plus 3620, 3640), not only 2599 against 3499 plus 3620, so a wrong subtotal in the export is caught.

## CK-11 Retained earnings roll-forward
- Rule: 3849 = 3660 + 3680 + 3700 + 3720 + 3740. 3660 "Retained earnings/deficit start"; 3680 "Net income/loss"; 3700 "Dividends declared" (includes liquidating dividends, redemption premium, stock dividends); 3720 "Prior period adjustments"; 3740 "Other items affecting retained earnings". [fact, S1] Schedule 100 itself shows only 3660, 3680, 3700, 3849 on its page 2; 3720 and 3740 exist in the GIFI index. [fact, S2 and S1]
- 3680 should equal 9999 (net income after taxes and extraordinary items); if 9998 is present, 3680 = 9999 plus or minus 9998. [fact, S1] The clause does not tie 3680 to 9999. Add it.
- The balance sheet retained-earnings line 3600 should equal 3849. [inference from S1/S2 layout: 3600 appears as the balance sheet retained earnings line and 3849 as the end of the roll-forward; the clause omits this tie.]
- Worked: 3660 = 40,000; 3680 = 55,000; 3700 = (8,000); 3720 = 0; 3740 = 0. 3849 = 40,000 + 55,000 - 8,000 = 87,000. If 3600 shows 87,000, pass.
- Sign edge: RC4088 says only "mark negative amounts using brackets or a minus sign"; it does not say whether 3700 is entered as a negative [S1, "not found"]. The clause's "with Schedule 100's signs" cannot be coded until the trial shows how Taxprep exports 3700. Safe design: store sign as a per-field rule in data, default dividends negative, and check that the result is invariant to the choice only when 3700 is zero. A deficit is a negative 3849. [inference]
- "3660 equals last year's 3849": correct when the prior year was not restated. After a restatement or a reassessment that changed book figures, the difference should land in 3720 (prior period adjustments) and the check should require 3660 = last year's 3849 as FILED, with the change in 3720. [inference]
- Edge: first year of a new corporation or an amalgamation: 3660 is zero or comes from the predecessors; the check needs an "opening" flag. [inference]

## CK-12 Opening balances equal last year's closing as assessed
- UCC by class: Schedule 8 column 2 = "the amount from column 13 of your last tax year's Schedule 8". [fact, S4 p.42 text, 2017] Class 14.1 opened in 2017 from the old eligible capital pool. [fact, S4]
- RDTOH: opening = RDTOH at end of previous year minus any dividend refund issued in the previous year; plus refundable Part I tax (line 450) and Part IV tax (Schedule 3 line 360); plus amalgamation or wind-up transfers (line 480 only). [fact, S4, 2017, single pool]. Since 2019 there are two pools (eligible and non-eligible); the 2017 guide cannot confirm the current line numbers. [not found for current form]
- GRIP: Schedule 53 determines it; CRA says to file it if an eligible dividend was paid or the GRIP balance changed so that "the GRIP balance on our records is correct". Balances can be viewed in My Business Account "View return balances". [fact, S4] The same service is stated for RDTOH. Capital dividend account and donations carried forward: the schedule pages were not opened, so nothing is claimed beyond: loss pools are on Schedule 4 and donations on Schedule 2 [fact, S4 section headings].
- Worked (UCC): class 8, last year's Schedule 8 col 13 = 12,400; this year's col 2 must be 12,400. Pass. If the notice of reassessment cut last year's CCA claim by 2,000 (UCC 14,400), col 2 must be 14,400 and the check must compare to the assessed figure.
- Edge, the main one: "as assessed". Last year's Taxprep file shows what the firm filed; CRA may have reassessed (S4: normal reassessment period and requests for change). CRA changes to CCA claimed need a written request within 90 days of the notice [fact, S4 p.43]. The check must therefore read the prior-year NOA or the CRA "view return balances" figures, never only the prior filed return; if only the filed return is available, the result is "unverified" (flag), not pass. [inference]
- Edge: amalgamation or wind-up (S4 line 480) breaks "opening = last closing"; needs a transfer flag.

## CK-13 Instalments claimed equal what CRA credited
- Rule: line 840 "Tax instalments paid". "If there is a discrepancy between the amount you report on the return and the interim balance in your business account, we will use the amount in your business account." [fact, S4 p.123] So CRA's own account figure wins.
- Worked: the return claims 840 = 14,000 (four payments of 3,500). My Business Account shows 12,250 (one payment missed or posted to the wrong period). CRA will assess on 12,250: the tie fails, and the balance due rises by 1,750.
- Edge: payments made after year end for this year's tax (balance due) are not instalments; instalment dates differ for a first-year corporation or one with a short year; a payment posted to a different program account (RC for payroll, GST) can be transferred. [S4: transfers within accounts of the same nine-digit business number] Interest offsets and refunds applied from the prior year appear in the account but are not instalments. [guess] The source for the credited amount must be a CRA account statement; not available from Taxprep.

## CK-14 Schedule 50
- Rule: private corporations complete Schedule 50 "for any shareholder who holds 10% or more of the corporation's common and/or preferred shares" (S7); the 2017 guide adds: "a maximum of the 10 top shareholders". [fact, S4 p.34] Percentages by class should sum to 100% only across ALL holders; Schedule 50 lists just the 10% holders, so the sum on the schedule can be below 100%.
- Clause wrong as written: "Schedule 50 holdings add to 100% by class" cannot be tested from Schedule 50 alone. Test instead: (holders on Schedule 50 by class) plus (the rest from the share register or the onboarding data) = 100%; and Schedule 50 percentages never exceed 100% by class. Also the clause's "every holder of 10% or more" is right; the 10-holder cap in the guide may bind when several classes exist. [inference]
- Worked: common 100 shares: Owner A 60, Holding Co B 30, Employee C 10. All three hold 10% or more: all listed; 60 + 30 + 10 = 100%. Preferred 100 shares: A 95, D 5: only A is listed (95%); the 5% holder is correctly absent and the Schedule 50 column then adds to 95%, not 100%.
- Edge: "10% or more" is by votes and value per class; indirect holders through a holding company are listed as the holding company only. [guess]

## CK-15 Schedule 1 add-backs
- Amortization: Sch 1 line 104 "Amortization of tangible assets" (S3). GIFI 8670 is "Amortization of tangible assets" (S1). Tie: line 104 = 8670. Intangible amortization goes on line 106 and natural resource on 105 [fact, S3]; the GIFI codes for those are not opened [not found], so 8670 alone will fail when the client also amortizes goodwill or incorporation costs. Note also 8670 may include leasehold improvements ("amortization of leasehold improvements and amounts referred to as depreciation") [fact, S1].
- CCA: Sch 1 line 403 "Capital cost allowance from Schedule 8" (S3). Tie 403 = Schedule 8 total CCA (column 12 total). [inference; the Schedule 8 form was not opened]. Also line 107 recapture and line 404 terminal loss are separate lines. [fact, S3]
- Meals: ITA s.67.1: amount deemed to be 50% of the lesser of actual and reasonable. [fact, S6] Schedule 1 line 121 "Non-deductible meals and entertainment expenses". Tie: line 121 = 50% of 8523 [fact, S1 for 8523]. 
- Penalties and interest: line 103 "Interest and penalties on taxes"; line 128 "Non-deductible fines and penalties under section 67.6". [fact, S3] The clause's "tax penalties and interest" needs both lines, plus line 101 and 102 (income tax provisions) if the books carry income tax expense. [fact, S3 lists them]
- Worked: books show 8670 = 18,000, CCA claimed 21,500, 8523 = 6,000, CRA interest 400. Line 104 = 18,000; line 403 = 21,500; line 121 = 3,000; line 103 = 400. Pass if all four match.
- Edge: 8523 includes items that are fully deductible: the 2017 guide notes 80% for long-haul truck drivers and full deduction for meals at a temporary construction work camp [fact, S4 p.36]; other fully deductible cases (staff events, items sold to customers) are in the Act but not opened [guess]. The check should flag a mismatch for a person rather than fail, and allow an override with a reason. The Taxprep GIFI export may also hold meals under another expense code. [guess]
- Edge: amortization of capitalized interest (line 119) and club dues (line 120) are separate add-backs, not in the clause.

## CK-18 Every figure recomputed from its sources equals its Taxprep cell
- This is the general rule, not a CRA rule; no source needed. Edges from above: rounding (Taxprep and CRA work in whole dollars) so compare after rounding the same way; compare signed values; ignore cells CRA says are computed from another schedule only after that schedule ties. [inference]

## CK-19 Associated group and the business limit
- Rule: associated CCPCs share the business limit: Schedule 23 column 5 percentages total at most 100%, column 6 = column 4 times column 5, and "Ensure that the total at line A does not exceed $500,000". [fact, S5] ITA 125(2): the limit is nil for an associated CCPC unless the agreement is filed (125(3)); if not filed within 30 days of the Minister's notice the Minister allocates (125(4)). [fact, S6]
- Short year: if the tax year is under 51 weeks, prorate the column 6 amount by days in the year divided by 365 and enter on line 410; 365 is not adjusted for a leap year. [fact, S5, S4 p.30-31, S6 125(5)(b)]
- Worked: Corp A and Corp B are associated; A gets 60% of 500,000 = 300,000, B gets 40% = 200,000, total 500,000. A's year runs 1 Jul to 31 Dec (184 days): 300,000 x 184 / 365 = 151,233 on line 410. Pass; B (full year) enters 200,000.
- Clause wrong or incomplete as written:
  1. "total no more than $500,000" is the maximum, but the allocated limit can be lower: it is reduced under 125(5.1) by the large corporation amount (line 415, 0.225% x (C - $10,000,000)) and by adjusted aggregate investment income (Schedule 7 lines 744 and 745). [fact, S5] Compare Schedule 23 to $500,000 only; compare line 410 (after proration) against column 6 times days/365. Provinces with their own limit (Nova Scotia, PEI, Saskatchewan reported higher) do not change the federal limit. [fact: canada.ca corporation tax rates page; figures not reconfirmed]
  2. Schedule 23 "identical on every return" is only true per calendar year of the agreement, and only for corporations with a tax year ending in that year; an amended agreement replaces the earlier one (S5 line 075). A corporation with two tax years ending in one calendar year files one agreement per year, and the second year's limit is the lesser of the first year's and the second year's allocation (125(5)). [fact, S5, S4 p.30] Example from the guide: 90,000 x 184/365 = 45,370.
  3. Percentages, not dollars, are what the associated returns must agree on; association codes 2 to 5 (third-corporation and non-CCPC cases) take a column 4 limit of 0 and no percentage. [fact, S5]
  4. A non-CCPC associate has no limit but still affects the group; do not require it to carry a percentage.

## Summary of clause changes this research supports
1. CK-10: add 3640 and the contra-asset sign rule. 2. CK-11: add 3680 = 9999 (or 9998 case) and 3600 = 3849; sign for 3700 to be fixed by the trial. 3. CK-12: define "as assessed" (NOA or CRA account, else flag). 4. CK-13: source is the CRA account, not the return. 5. CK-14: cannot sum to 100% from Schedule 50 alone. 6. CK-15: add lines 103 and 128, intangible lines, and meals exceptions. 7. CK-19: reduction under 125(5.1), amendments, two-year rule.
