Item 39 is right. Confidence: high on the 3,000 limit; medium on class 14.1 detail.

## Item 40 and 41 Amounts due from shareholder; personal spending
- [fact] ITA 15(2): a loan or debt to a shareholder (or person connected) is included in the shareholder's income for the year, except 15(2.6): repaid within one year after the end of the lender corporation's taxation year and not part of a series of loans and repayments; 15(2.4) employee exception (not by reason of shareholding). https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-3.3/section-15.html (opened twice; summary text for 15(2) opening was garbled once, take the 15(2.6) and 15(2.4) points as medium confidence)
- [fact] ITA 15(1): benefit conferred on a shareholder is included in the shareholder's income. Same page. ITA 15(9): 80.4 deemed interest is treated as a 15(1) benefit. ITA 80.4(2) deemed interest benefit on shareholder loan at the prescribed rate less interest paid within 30 days after year end. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-3.3/section-80.4.html (opened)
- [fact] ITA 6(1)(a): value of benefits received because of employment is income; shareholder-employees are covered by 6(1)(a) if the benefit is by reason of employment, 15(1) if by reason of shareholding. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-3.3/section-6.html (opened)

## Item 42 T4A and T5018 for subcontractors
- [fact] T5018 required if all of: primary business income more than 50% from construction; payments to Canadian-resident subcontractors for construction services; total to a subcontractor over 500 in the calendar year excluding GST/HST; else a line-by-line listing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/completing-filing-information-returns/t5018-slip-statement-contract-payments.html (opened). Filing deadline and penalty not on that page.
- [fact] T4A box 048 fees for services: total payments over 500 in a calendar year to other businesses, T4A by the last day of February. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-fees-for-service/fact-sheet-reporting-fees-services.html (opened). The page says the requirement applies to payments to other businesses and singles out trucking CCPCs; it does not settle every case of individuals.
- Answer: yes, a check (or new clause) is justified for a construction-type client: a flag when payments to one subcontractor exceed 500 and no T5018 listing exists, plus a 048 flag for non-construction clients. A person confirms the 50% test. Confidence: medium (T4A scope for individual contractors not settled here).
