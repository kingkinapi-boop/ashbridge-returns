## Worked examples a CPA can check (all made up)

1. CK-30: lender year end 31 Dec 2025; advance 40,000 on 10 Jun 2025; repaid 25,000 on 15 Mar 2026; deadline 31 Dec 2026; 15,000 open then: 15,000 is the borrower's 2025 income; 20(1)(j) deduction when later repaid (A).
2. CK-30 FIFO: advances 30,000 (15 Mar 2025) and 12,000 (2 Sep 2025); 30,000 repaid 20 Dec 2026 clears the March advance; 12,000 flagged (B).
3. CK-31: loan 30,000 on 1 Mar 2025; repaid by cheque 20 Dec 2025; re-borrowed 28,000 on 8 Jan 2026: flag (28,000 >= 50% of 30,000, 19 days). Same repayment by a 30,000 dividend credit: no flag (1.86).
4. CK-32 (Folio S3-F1-C2 2.25): 100,000 at 0.75%; rates 1% two quarters, 1.25% two quarters: 500 + 625 = 1,125; interest paid 500 in year + 250 within 30 days = 750; benefit 375.
5. CK-33: year end 31 Dec 2025; day 180 = 29 Jun 2026 (Jan 31 + Feb 28 + Mar 31 + Apr 30 + May 31 + 29). Bonus 20,000 paid 29 Jun 2026: in time. Paid 15 Jul 2026 (day 196): deduct in FY2026; 2025 taxable income up 20,000.
6. CK-36: AAII 75,000 gives passive cut 5 x 25,000 = 125,000 (CRA page). Prior-year taxable capital 30M gives B = 0.225% x 20M = 45,000; capital cut 500,000 x 45,000 / 90,000 = 250,000. Greater = 250,000: federal limit 250,000. Ontario limit uses the capital cut only: 250,000 (if Ontario's phase-out is the same straight line: CPA check).
7. CK-37: US shares at cost 130,000 at any time in the year: T1135 Part A with the T2. Management fees of 1,200,000 paid to a non-resident sister company: T106.
8. CK-38: bonus accrued 31 Dec 2025, paid 15 Jan 2026: on the 2026 T4, not 2025. Dividend 30,000 paid in 2025 with no T5 by end of Feb 2026: flag.
9. CK-41: closing GRIP 40,000; eligible dividends 55,000; excess 15,000; Part III.1 tax 3,000 unless the 185.1(2) election. Capital dividend 50,000 with CDA 30,000: excess 20,000 x 3/5 = 12,000 unless the 184(3) election.

## Where the reports disagreed

- 80.4(2) scope: A's fetch said "non-resident". Wrong: the Act excludes only corporations resident in Canada (and all-corporate partnerships); Folio 2.2 agrees. B was right.
- CK-33 deadline: B "pay by day 179"; A "day 180". A is right: payment on day 180 is in time (IT-109R2 para 10). B's "day 179" is a safe habit, not the rule.
- CK-33 admin relief where source deductions were remitted (B, guess): not in IT-109R2. Dropped.
- CK-31 window: A same fiscal year and 50%; B 60 days within 10%. Neither has a source; CRA gives no period. Settled as amber 180 days and 50% (broader than B, flag for a person); open for the CPA.
- CK-35 one-client share: A 90%, B 80%. Both are guesses; 80% chosen (flag rather than silent pass).
- CK-36 capital range: CRA's Budget 2018 page still says 10M to 15M; the Act divisor (90,000) and the Ontario page give 10M to 50M. The Act wins; the CRA page is stale.
- T106 and T1134 thresholds: B "not found"; A found them; both confirmed on the Act.
