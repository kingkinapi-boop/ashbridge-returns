## 1. T2 review checklist, small Ontario CCPC (reviewing CPA order)

No CRA or CCH page publishes a reviewer checklist [not found]. The order below follows the T2 return's own sequence [fact, CRA3, CRA4: page 3 net income and Schedule 1, page 4 small business deduction and Schedule 23] and common practice [inference].
1. Engagement and identity: legal name, BN, year-end, fiscal period within 53 weeks, prior-year return and notice of assessment agree (opening balances, loss balances, GRIP, RDTOH, CDA from Auto-fill). Inactive corporation exemption from 100, 125, 141 only if truly inactive [fact, CRA3].
2. Page 1 and page 2 questions: CCPC status, Ontario permanent establishment, associated corporations (Schedule 23 if associated, Schedule 9 related and associated), jurisdiction, books location, change of control or year-end.
3. Financial statements and GIFI: Schedules 100, 125, 141 present; same line detail as the statements, not subtotals; assets = liabilities + equity; revenue - expenses = net income [fact, CRA2 and prior file]. Check 141 preparer code (audit, review, compilation).
4. Books tie: TB to GIFI, bank and loan reconciliations, HST/GST and payroll control accounts, subledgers, year-end cut-off, accruals and prepaid, shareholder accounts (part 2).
5. Schedule 1: add-backs (amortization, meals 50%, non-deductible items, reserves, provision), deductions (CCA, ITA deductions), tie to book net income. Schedule 8 CCA: additions, AII, disposals, half-year, class correctness, vehicle limits (class 10.1).
6. Other income and loss schedules: Schedule 6 dispositions, 4 losses (continuity, carry back), 13 reserves, 3 dividends received, 7 aggregate investment income (passive income and grind of the business limit).
7. Tax computation: small business deduction on active business income, business limit $500,000 shared by association (Schedule 23) [fact, CRA4 lines 400 to 430], general rate otherwise, PSB 5% additional tax if applicable [fact, CRA5], Ontario Schedule 500 and its credits, RDTOH, dividend refund, part IV tax.
8. Pools and balances roll forward: GRIP/LRIP (Sch 53), RDTOH (Sch 3, Sch 55 type), CDA, loss pools, all reconciled to Auto-fill and the prior NOA.
9. Shareholder and related-party schedules: Schedule 50 shareholders, Schedule 11 or T2 questions on transactions with shareholders, T5 and T4 slips match dividends and salary, T106 if non-arm's-length non-resident, T1134 and T1135 foreign-reporting questions on page 2/3 of the T2.
10. Balance and instalments: balance due, instalments paid (monthly vs quarterly eligibility and the $3,000 test per the prior file), payments by due date, interest and penalties, balance-due day (2 or 3 months).
11. Filing: Taxprep diagnostics cleared, e-file eligibility, T183CORP signed, copy to client. [inference] The review order is practice, not a CRA text.

## 2. Owner-manager issues list (issue: data that signals it)

[inference] unless a URL is shown. Where a CRA page was not opened, it says so.
- Shareholder loans: 1300/1301/2180 (due from shareholder) with a balance at year-end; 2780/3260 (due to shareholder) with a negative balance (shareholder account overdrawn); a credit balance cleared by journal at year-end. Rule: loan not repaid within one year after the corporation's year-end is income under 15(2) unless an exception applies; repayment cannot be part of a series of loans and repayments (CRA folio S3-F1-C1; result snippets only, folio not read in full) [fact as to the title, inference to the detail]. Also deemed interest benefit (80.4) if no or low interest.
- Remuneration mix: salary vs dividend split; no payroll account but personal RRSP room needs; dividends declared without a minute; eligible vs non-eligible designation; T4/T5 totals do not match GL accounts 9060/3700.
- Personal expenses: owner's credit card, groceries, home mortgage, personal insurance, travel in the books; "other" and meals accounts; cash withdrawals coded to expense. Resolve as shareholder benefit (15(1)) or shareholder loan.
- Vehicles: motor vehicle expense and CCA class 10.1 (cost cap), no logbook, personal use, standby charge and operating benefit on a corporate vehicle (T4 box 34 and 40 or ITA 6(1)(e)), GST/HST ITC reduction.
- Home office: rent paid to the shareholder, utilities, property tax; usually a shareholder expense claim, not corporate; if corporate rent is paid, check lease and T776-type income to the owner.
- CDA: capital dividend account increases from non-taxable half of capital gains, life insurance proceeds; signals: a capital disposal (Schedule 6), insurance proceeds, T2054 election before the dividend; CDA balance in Auto-fill vs working papers.
- RDTOH and dividend refunds: investment income (interest, rent, taxable capital gains) in Schedule 7; part IV tax on portfolio dividends; non-eligible RDTOH refunded only when non-eligible dividends are paid; eligible RDTOH when eligible dividends, or non-eligible when no non-eligible RDTOH remains (search snippet of a practitioner page, Mondaq; not CRA) [inference].
- Association: another corporation owned by the same person or family, shared business limit (Schedule 23 allocation), Schedule 9 relationships, Schedule 28 election; signal: a shareholder with other holdco, spouse companies [fact as to schedules, CRA4].
- PSB/SIB: one client pays most revenue, no employees, shareholder 10% or more; five conditions (through a corporation, specified shareholder, five or fewer full-time employees, payer not associated, would be an employee if no corporation) [fact, CRA5]; SIB is a specified investment business: rental or investment income with five or fewer full-time employees, loses the small business deduction and gets higher refundable tax [inference; CRA SIB page not opened].
- Instalments: prior-year tax over $3,000 triggers instalments; CCPC may qualify for quarterly; bank debits to CRA coded to expense or to the shareholder account instead of 2680; interest on late instalments [fact, in prior file CRA21].

## 4. Taxprep diagnostics: ignorable vs e-file blockers

Types, per Taxprep help (TP1, ten rows): Mandatory modification ("enter or modify data so the return is not rejected"), Missing information, Error ("review and correct if necessary"), Possible input error, Filing ("verify the list of forms to file"), Review (overridden cells), Optimization, Tax planning possibilities, Exclusion ("reasons the bar codes cannot be printed"), Filters and diagnostics (custom) [fact, TP1]. iFirm Taxprep uses Error, Warning, Information, Filing error and Filing warning: "Filing errors: modify data to be eligible for electronic transmission"; "Filing warnings: the return is eligible for electronic transmission, but verify" [fact, CCH1].
- Blockers [fact]: errors in the T2 EFILE tab, "errors that can cause the return to be rejected"; not all are in that tab, so general diagnostics must also be checked [fact, TP2]; Mandatory modification; Filing errors; Exclusion (bar codes cannot print) [inference that it blocks e-file, TP1 does not say it explicitly]; CRA e-file rejection codes (format, business number and incorporation date, amendment restrictions, attachment types such as pdf, xls) [fact, TP4].
- Ignorable [inference]: Optimization, Tax planning possibilities, Review, Information, Filing warning (after verifying). Diagnostics can be marked Reviewed, Ignored or Hidden for one return or all returns [fact, TP3, CCH1]; hiding is not the same as fixing.
- Not found: Taxprep's own statement of which diagnostic types may be ignored; a page listing the 3,000+ diagnostics with severity. The ignore decision for Error, Missing information and Possible input error is a preparer or CPA judgement, not a Taxprep rule. [guess] Build the policy as: block on Mandatory modification, Missing information, Filing error, Exclusion and T2 EFILE tab; require a named person to disposition Error and Possible input error; allow optimization and planning to be ignored in bulk; log every ignored one with who and when.
