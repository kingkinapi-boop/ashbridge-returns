## Findings that change the clause text (most important first)

1. CK-15 amortization add-back is wrong as written. [fact, S1] Taxprep transfers "Amortization of tangible assets" to Schedule 1 line 104 as 8670 + 8459 + 9791, not 8670 alone. Schedule 8 REC pulls Schedule 1 lines 104 + 105 + 106 as its book depreciation [fact, S3]. A tie of line 104 = 8670 raises a false exception whenever 8459 or 9791 is used, and ignores lines 105 and 106 (natural resource, intangible amortization). Fix: add-back = 8670 + 8459 + 9791 (confirm the set against the 2026 Taxprep help in the spike, since S1 is 2019).
2. CK-15 meals: [fact, S1] Taxprep transfers "50% of 8523" to Schedule 1 line 121, and only when a checkbox "Line 8523 of the GIFI" is selected. [inference] The tie "half of 8523 is added back" is right, but a preparer who leaves the box off gets no add-back and no balance error, so this is a real miss the check catches. False alarm: meals not limited to 50% (some special cases) once the preparer has split them; allow a documented override, not a hard fail. [guess] on which cases; verify against ITA 67.1 before coding.
3. CK-19 51 weeks: [fact, S4, S5] a year shorter than 51 weeks prorates the business limit by days in the year / 365 (ITA 125(5)(b)). The shares are identical across the group but the dollar limit differs by year length. [inference] A check demanding identical dollars would false-fail; check the percentages total at most 100% (dollars at most $500,000 before proration) and recompute the prorated dollars per return. Not found: how associated corporations with different year-ends are handled beyond the agreement. [fact, S5] Where no agreement is filed, the Minister allocates (125(4)), so a missing Schedule 23 is a flag. A sister return not yet in the system should flag, not fail.
4. CK-12 opening balances after a reassessment: [fact, S6] Auto-fill gives loss closing balances from "the last return assessed/reassessed" and says they "do not include outstanding assessment activity"; it also supplies ERDTOH/NERDTOH, GRIP and CDA. [inference] The tie is right in principle, but the baseline must be the latest assessed figure, not last year's filed figure. A reassessment changes the opening with no change in the books. Rule: store the source of each opening balance (filed, assessed, reassessed, Auto-fill pull date) and flag when a newer assessment exists than the one used. [fact, S8] CRA offers reassessment on request; the page gives nothing on carry-forward timing (not found). Zero difference on a file with a pending reassessment is not proof.
5. CK-11 retained earnings: [fact, S2] 3680 (net income) must match 9999 on Schedule 125; 3849 must equal 3600 when both are reported. [fact, S1] Taxprep tests 3849 > 0 or < 0 when transferring (Schedule 33), so a deficit is a negative 3849. [inference] The clause formula with Schedule 100's signs false-fails if dividends in 3700 are entered as positives and misses a real break if both signs are flipped consistently. Add the two sibling ties from S2: 3680 = 9999, and 3849 = 3600. 3660 versus last year's 3849 is a false alarm when prior statements were restated (3720 or a reassessment); require an explanation line rather than zero. Not found: Taxprep's own diagnostic wording for this tie.
6. CK-13 and CK-14: not found in any page opened. [fact, S7, weak] Schedule 50 review is only described as confirming shareholder details, especially owner-managers. [fact, S6] Auto-fill excludes "amounts not yet applied, or held credits", so a late-year payment often shows as missing. Do not code a rule from this report; treat an instalment timing difference as a flag showing the payment date.

## Per tie: where it usually breaks, false alarms, worked example

### CK-10 Balance sheet balances
- Breaks [fact, S2]: the most common filing error is 2599 not equal to 3499 + 3620; CRA validates it first. Accumulated amortization must be negative, "(4,000)" not "4,000" [fact, S2]. [inference] Other causes: shareholder loan debit entered as a positive liability; current-year earnings not rolled into equity.
- False alarm [inference]: GIFI is whole dollars [fact, S2], so rounding can leave a gap of a few dollars; allow a stated tolerance of one dollar per rounded line.
- Example: 2599 = 410,000; 3499 = 260,000; 3620 = 150,000: ties. If accumulated amortization of 40,000 is entered as +40,000, assets read 490,000 and the gap is 80,000, twice the entry: a gap of exactly twice some line points to a sign error.

### CK-11 Retained earnings roll
- Example: 3660 = 100,000; 3680 = 60,000; 3700 = (25,000); 3849 = 135,000. With dividends entered as +25,000 the formula gives 185,000 and fails; with the sign rule it ties. Last year's 3849 was 100,000, so 3660 ties.

### CK-12 Opening balances
- Breaks [inference]: UCC by class after a reassessed disposition or class change; loss pools after a carryback applied post-filing; RDTOH eligible/non-eligible split after reassessment; donations carried forward as the oldest year expires [guess].
- False alarm: comparing with the filed figure when CRA assessed another (finding 4).
- Example: last year's filed closing non-capital loss 40,000; CRA reassessed to 32,000. Opening must be 32,000. A tie to the filed 40,000 passes the wrong number; the tie to the last-assessed figure raises the right flag.

### CK-13 Instalments
- Breaks [inference]: payments applied to another year; payment made on the last day but posted next day; refunds or transfers between years.
- Example: claimed 12,000 over four payments of 3,000; CRA statement credits 9,000 because the last payment posted on day 2 of the next year. Flag with the payment date, not an error.

### CK-14 Schedule 50
- [inference] Breaks: percentages entered of total shares rather than by class; a holding company shown without its own owners; rounding to 99.99 or 100.01. Allow a stated rounding tolerance. The 10% test is "at or above": Class A of 1,000 shares, X 500, Y 400, Z 100: Z is exactly 10% and must be listed.

### CK-15 Schedule 1 add-backs
- See findings 1 and 2. Practitioner view [fact, S7]: add back book amortization and deduct CCA from Schedule 8. Breaks [fact, S3]: for Class 14 property Taxprep "is not able to reflect the accounting impact" and "cannot highlight all discrepancies". False alarm [inference]: claiming less than maximum CCA is a choice, so the tie is "claimed equals Schedule 8 total", never "equals maximum".
- Penalties and interest add-back: no help page opened names the line. [inference] A bank charge and a CRA penalty can sit in the same GIFI code, so code cannot tell them apart; make it a flag with a question to the preparer, not a computed tie.
- Example: 8670 = 18,000; 8459 = 2,000; 9791 = 0: line 104 should be 20,000; Schedule 8 CCA 14,500; 8523 = 6,000, line 121 = 3,000. A check using 8670 alone expects 18,000 and wrongly fails.

### CK-18 Recompute equals Taxprep cell
- [fact, S3] Taxprep says its reconciliation cannot highlight all discrepancies and some amounts need manual adjustment. [inference] Mismatches in the Class 14 type cells are known false alarms; a preparer override is the other. Record "override present" with its source instead of failing.
- Example: recomputed Part I tax 24,500, cell 24,500: ties. Cell 24,000 after a hand-entered rate: exception naming the override.

### CK-19 Schedule 23
- See finding 3. Example: two CCPCs share 70% and 30%. A has a full year to 31 Dec: limit 350,000. B started 1 Oct, first year to 31 Dec = 92 days: 150,000 x 92 / 365 = 37,808. Both carry 70/30 and dollars differ. Percentages totalling at most 100% passes; identical dollars would wrongly fail B.
