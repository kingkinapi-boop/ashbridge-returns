## Ties

1. **CK-10 Balance sheet.** GIFI 2599 = 3499 + 3620, and 3640 = 2599, contra-asset lines negative. Source: RC4088. Example: assets 410,000 (equipment 60,000, accumulated amortization (18,000) included); liabilities 150,000; equity 260,000; 3640 410,000: pass. Accumulated amortization entered +18,000 gives assets 446,000: fail, the 36,000 gap is twice one line, labelled "likely sign error". Question: right?
2. **CK-11 Retained earnings.** 3849 = 3660 + 3680 + 3700 + 3720 + 3740 with 3700 negative; 3680 = 9999; 3849 = 3600; 3660 = last year's filed 3849, restatements in 3720. Source: RC4088. Example: 3660 100,000; 3680 60,000 (= 9999); 3700 (25,000); 3849 135,000 = 3600: pass. Dividends entered +25,000 give 185,000: fail. Question: right, and is a restatement always in 3720?
3. **CK-12 Openings.** Losses, RDTOH, GRIP and CDA open at the latest assessed figure (Auto-fill); UCC and donations at last year's filed closing, flagged if a reassessment is on file. Source: T4012 ch. 3; About T2 Auto-fill. Example: last year's filed non-capital loss 40,000, reassessed to 32,000; Auto-fill shows 32,000: opening must be 32,000. Question: right?
4. **CK-14 Schedule 50.** Every holder of 10% or more (exactly 10% included), at most ten, at the register's percentage; each column totals at most 100%. Source: T2 Schedule 50; T4012 ch. 2. Example: 1,000 common shares: X 500, Y 400, Z 100; all three listed (Z exactly 10%); column 400 totals 100%. Question: right?
5. **CK-15 Schedule 1 amortization.** Line 104 = 8670 + 8459 (+ 9791), not 8670 alone. Source: T2 Schedule 1; Taxprep GIFI transfer list. Example: 8670 18,000; 8459 2,000: line 104 must be 20,000. Question: right?
6. **CK-15 Meals.** Line 121 = 50% of 8523 less amounts excepted under ITA 67.1(2), each exception flagged. Source: ITA 67.1. Example: 8523 6,000, of which 1,000 is a staff party under 67.1(2)(f): line 121 = 50% x 5,000 = 2,500, and the 1,000 is flagged. Plain case: 8523 3,000, no exceptions: add-back 1,500. Question: right?
7. **CK-19 Associated group.** Same Schedule 23 percentages on every group return for the calendar year; line A at most 500,000; a year under 51 weeks prorates by days/365. Source: T2 Schedule 23; ITA 125(2) to (5). Example: A and B associated, 70/30. A full year: 350,000. B's first year 1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026, 92 days: 150,000 x 92 / 365 = 37,808. Same percentages, different dollars: pass. Question: right?
8. **CK-43 Shareholder-loan continuity.** Last year's closing loan + advances - repayments = year-end balance of that shareholder's loan accounts. Source: Folio S3-F1-C1. Example: opening debit 12,000; advances 8,000; repayments 5,000: the loan accounts must show a 15,000 debit at year end. Question: right?

## Reconciliations

9. **CK-13 Instalments.** Line 840 against what CRA's account credits to the year, with typed items (not yet applied, applied to another year, transferred from another account). Source: T4012 (2017 edition, line 840; not reconfirmed in 2025); About T2 Auto-fill. Example: line 840 12,000; CRA account 9,000; one 3,000 payment dated 31 Dec, applied 2 Jan: reconciled with item R23, remainder zero. Question: right to make this a reconciliation rather than a tie?
10. **CK-20 Revenue against GST/HST line 101 (regular method).** Line 101 includes zero-rated and exempt supplies, interest and capital-asset proceeds, excludes the tax. Source: GST/HST return instructions. Example: books 520,000 = taxable fees 480,000 + zero-rated 25,000 + interest 3,000 + gain on equipment 12,000 (proceeds 20,000). Line 101 = 480,000 + 25,000 + 3,000 + 20,000 = 528,000. Difference -8,000 = R10 (gain 12,000 against proceeds 20,000). Unexplained 0. Question: does line 101 really include interest and capital-asset proceeds?
11. **CK-20 Quick method.** Line 101 includes the tax, excludes zero-rated supplies. Source: RC4058. Example: books 105,000 = taxable fees 100,000 + zero-rated 5,000. Line 101 = 100,000 x 1.13 = 113,000. Difference -8,000 = R07 (-13,000) + R08 (+5,000). Unexplained 0. Question: right?
12. **CK-21 OHIP window.** RAs count up to the close of the three-month submission window plus one monthly cycle after year end. Source: Ontario claims submission pages. Example: 31 Dec year end; a December service can be paid as late as about 15 May. RAs 488,000 + R02 71,000 - R03 39,000 = 520,000 = books. Books 524,000 would leave 4,000 unexplained and flagged. Question: is about 15 May the right close for a December year end?
13. **CK-24 Dividends credited to a shareholder loan.** Is a dividend credited to the shareholder's loan account, with no cash, "paid" for the T5? Source: not found on the opened CRA pages. Example: 20,000 taxable dividend declared and credited to account 2781 on 15 Dec 2025, no cash moves: the system expects it on the 2025 T5 box 10 (reconciling item R14 on CK-24). Question: right?
14. **CK-4 The 29 reconciling-item types** (table in blueprint/05-checks.md, R01 to R29). Source: recs research; practice. Example (CK-22): bank 48,200 + R04 deposit 3,100 - R04 cheques 5,450 = 45,850; books 45,895 - R06 fee 45 = 45,850; unexplained 0. Question: any type missing, or any you would never accept? Is a $1 rounding allowance (CK-50) right?
15. **CK-44 CCA additions.** Schedule 8 additions by class against QBO capital-asset debits, with input tax credits, not-available-for-use items, disposals and expensed items as reconciling items. Source: T2 Schedule 8. Example: Schedule 8 class 50 addition 4,000; QBO debit to computer equipment 4,520 including HST 520 recovered as an ITC: R26 520, remainder 0. Question: right?
16. **CK-45 Income tax provision.** 9990 against total tax payable on the review lines; 2680 against that tax less instalments. Source: RC4088. Example: Part I tax 18,000, Part IV tax 4,000, dividend refund 3,000; 9990 18,000; instalments 12,000. Compared with Part I only, 9990 ties and 2680 should be 6,000; compared with total payable (18,000 + 4,000 - 3,000 = 19,000), 9990 differs by 1,000 and 2680 should be 7,000. Question: do the dividend refund and Part IV tax belong in the comparison?

## Flags

17. **CK-30 Shareholder loan, one-year rule.** A loan not repaid within one year after the end of the lender's tax year in which it was made is the borrower's income for the year of the loan; repayments go to the oldest loan first. Source: ITA 15(2), 15(2.6); Folio S3-F1-C1 1.71, 1.81. Example (FIFO): advances 30,000 (15 Mar 2025) and 12,000 (2 Sep 2025), lender year end 31 Dec; 30,000 repaid 20 Dec 2026 clears the March advance; 12,000 flagged at 31 Dec 2026. Example (single loan): advance 40,000 on 10 Jun 2025; 25,000 repaid 15 Mar 2026; 15,000 open at 31 Dec 2026 is the borrower's 2025 income, deductible under 20(1)(j) when repaid. Question: the FIFO default, and may a net shareholder credit offset a debit?
18. **CK-30 with the interest benefit.** Example: 1301 debit 20,000 at 31 Dec 2025, 5,000 of it lent in 2025, repaid 15 Nov 2026 with no re-borrowing: no 15(2) inclusion, but the 80.4 benefit on the interest-free period still applies. Not repaid by 31 Dec 2026: the 5,000 lent in 2025 is the shareholder's 2025 income; the flag date is 31 Dec 2026. Source: Folio S3-F1-C1 1.2, 1.20, 1.71. Question: right?
19. **CK-31 Series of loans and repayments.** Firm parameters: a cash repayment followed within 180 days by new advances of at least 50% of it; a repayment by dividend, salary or bonus credit is not a series. Source: Folio S3-F1-C1 1.84 to 1.86 (no period in days given). Example: loan 30,000 on 1 Mar 2025; repaid by cheque 20 Dec 2025; re-borrowed 28,000 on 8 Jan 2026: flag (28,000 is at least 15,000, 19 days later). The same repayment by a 30,000 dividend credit: no flag. Question: are 50% and 180 days right?
20. **CK-32 Interest benefit.** Prescribed-rate interest less interest paid in the year or within 30 days after; flagged over 250 (firm floor); not raised when 15(2) includes the loan. Source: ITA 80.4(2), 80.4(3)(b); Folio S3-F1-C2 2.25. Example: 100,000 at 0.75%; prescribed 1% for two quarters and 1.25% for two: 500 + 625 = 1,125; interest paid 500 in the year + 250 within 30 days = 750; benefit 375: flagged. Question: the 250 floor, and must the flag also prompt a T4 or T4A benefit entry?
21. **CK-34 Specified investment business.** Firm proxy: property income at least 50% of gross revenue and five or fewer full-time employees throughout the year. Source: ITA 125(7). Example: rent 180,000 of 300,000 revenue (60%), three full-time staff: flagged, no small business deduction unless a person decides otherwise. Question: the 50% proxy; does five full-time plus part-time staff meet "more than five"; what counts as full-time?
22. **CK-35 Personal services business.** Firm sign: one client gives at least 80% of revenue, owner holds 10% or more, five or fewer full-time employees, payer not associated. Source: ITA 125(7), 123.5, 18(1)(p). Example: an IT consultant's company, owner 100%, no staff, 85% of revenue from one bank: flagged with a note on client count, control of the work and staff. Question: is 80% right?
23. **CK-36 Business limit grind.** Passive cut 5 x (AAII - 50,000), nil limit at 150,000; capital cut 500,000 x (0.225% x (taxable capital - 10M)) / 90,000, nil at 50M; the greater applies; Ontario uses the capital cut only. Source: ITA 125(5.1); T4012 ch. 4; CRA Ontario SBD page. Example A: AAII 80,000: cut 150,000, limit 350,000. Example B: AAII 75,000 gives cut 125,000; prior-year taxable capital 30M gives 500,000 x 45,000 / 90,000 = 250,000; greater 250,000: federal limit 250,000; Ontario limit 250,000 if its phase-out is the same straight line. Question: right, including Ontario's shape?
24. **CK-37 Foreign reporting.** T1135 over 100,000 cost at any time; T106 over 1,000,000 with non-arm's-length non-residents; T1134 for any foreign affiliate. Source: ITA 233.3, 233.1(4), 233.4(4), 95(1); T1135 Q&A. Example: US shares at cost 130,000 at any time in the year: T1135 Part A. Management fees of 1,200,000 to a non-resident sister company: T106. Question: is there dormant-affiliate relief for the T1134, and what is the T1135 late penalty (practice says 25 a day to 2,500, not verified)?
25. **CK-38 Slips by year paid.** T4 for remuneration over 500 or with deductions, T5 for dividends of 50 or more, both by calendar year of payment. Source: RC4120; T5 guide. Example: bonus accrued 31 Dec 2025, paid 15 Jan 2026: on the 2026 T4, not 2025. Dividend 30,000 paid in 2025 with no T5 by the end of February 2026: flag. Question: the last-day-of-February due date and the penalty tiers?
26. **CK-39 Year-over-year movement (firm rule).** Flag a change of 25% or more and over the greater of 2,000 and 5% of revenue, with no preparer note. Source: none; firm policy. Example: revenue 400,000, floor 20,000. Office 8,000 to 12,000 (+50%, +4,000): no flag. Travel 30,000 to 55,000 (+83%, +25,000): flag. Question: are 25%, 2,000 and 5% of revenue right?
27. **CK-41 Excess eligible dividend and capital dividend.** Eligible dividends above GRIP at year end: 20% Part III.1 tax unless the 185.1(2) election; a capital dividend above CDA: 3/5 tax unless the 184(3) election. Source: ITA 89(1), 185.1, 83(2), 184(2). Example: closing GRIP 40,000; eligible dividends 55,000; excess 15,000; tax 3,000. Capital dividend 50,000 with CDA 30,000: 20,000 x 3/5 = 12,000. Question: right, and what is the 83(3) late-election penalty?
28. **CK-46 Instalments.** None when tax payable is 3,000 or less for either the current or the previous year. Source: CRA instalment requirements and dates. Example: 2024 2,800 and 2025 9,000: none required for 2025. 2024 4,000 and 2025 estimate 3,500: required. Question: right, and the quarterly conditions for a small CCPC?
29. **CK-47 Remuneration unpaid on day 180.** Accrued remuneration not paid by the end of day 180 after year end is deductible only when paid; payment on day 180 is in time; a promissory note is not payment. Source: ITA 78(4); IT-109R2 paras 10, 15. Example: year end 31 Dec 2025; day 180 = 29 Jun 2026. Bonus 20,000 paid 29 Jun 2026: in time. Paid 15 Jul 2026 (day 196): deductible in 2026; 2025 taxable income up 20,000. Question: is the day-180 reading right, and does a credit to the shareholder's account count as payment?

## Dates and mapping

30. **FLOW-12 Filing due date.** Six months after year end, the same day, or the last day of the sixth month when the year ends on a month's last day. Source: T4012, before you start. Example: year end 28 Feb 2026: due 31 Aug 2026. Year end 15 Mar 2026: due 15 Sep 2026. Question: right?
31. **TB-12 Never map to a GIFI total code.** A trial-balance account mapped to a calculated total code (such as 2599 or 9368) is refused. Source: RC4088. Example: a "Miscellaneous" account mapped to 9368 Total expenses is refused; it goes to 9270 Other expenses. Question: right?
32. **TB-3 Mapping by sign.** A shareholder account maps by its year-end sign, not its name. Source: RC4088 (1300/1301 due from, 2780/2781 due to). Example: an account in credit 4,000 in March and in debit 2,500 at year end maps to 1301, not 2781. Question: right?

## Added 1 Oct 2026 (evening): rounding

33. **RT-25 Rounding to whole dollars.** Each GIFI code is rounded to whole dollars, half away from zero. 3680 is set to the net income of the rounded Schedule 125 codes, so 3680 equals 9999. The rounding difference that keeps Schedule 100 balanced goes on the liability line with the largest amount, never on 3600, 3849 or share capital, so 3849 still equals 3600; it shows in the trace as rounding. Source: RC4088 (whole dollars; CK-11 ties). Example: books give cash 10,000.40, receivables 5,000.40, payables 4,000.30, loan 6,000.30, share capital 100, retained earnings 4,900.20. Rounded: assets 15,000; liabilities 10,000; equity 5,000 (100 + 4,900); balanced, no plug. If rounding leaves assets 1 dollar above, the 1 dollar goes on the loan (6,001), named "rounding". Question: right, and is a liability line the right place for the dollar?

Zo, 2 Oct 2026: "33: right" (decision 0023). RT-25 stands as written; the dollar goes on the largest liability line.

34. **RV-2 Federal tax on the approval summary.** The `federal_tax` row the client sees (T11's approval summary, and the same number in the CPA's brief, V02) is the total federal tax payable review line: Part I plus Part IV and other parts, less the dividend refund, not Part I alone. Until you answer, the build maps it to Part I tax with the row marked `cpa_check: pending`. Source: CK-45 (item 16); T2 return summary. Example: Part I tax 18,000, Part IV tax 4,000, dividend refund 3,000: the summary shows federal tax 19,000, not 18,000. A CCPC with no Part IV tax and no refund: Part I 18,000 shows 18,000 either way. Question: right?
35. **FLOW-8 Notice of assessment against the filed return.** T13 compares each line of CRA's notice with the same review line of the filed return through `data/assessment/notice-lines.json` (for example, the notice's Part I tax with the filed Part I tax, and the notice's balance owing with the filed balance owing or refund); a notice line with no mapping is shown to a person as "not on the filed return", never skipped. The full mapping is listed here when the test world's notice shape (W29) is fixed. Source: FLOW-8; T4012. Example: filed Part I tax 18,000.00; notice Part I tax 18,000.01: one difference of 0.01 and a follow-up item. Question: right, and which notice lines should be compared?

36. **RT-15 Cells T2 Auto-fill writes need no other source.** Cells Taxprep's T2 Auto-fill writes from CRA's assessed data sit on the allowed-typing list as "imported from CRA", so they need no source; a cell Returns imported keeps its own class; where Returns holds CRA's figure, a different value in the export is an override and needs a reason. Source: About T2 Auto-fill; trial day 4 (reference/taxprep/FINDINGS.md 3e). Example: Auto-fill writes an eligible RDTOH balance of 12,000; the export shows 12,000: imported from CRA, no source asked; the preparer changes it to 11,500: override, reason required. Question: is CRA's Auto-fill data enough of a source for every cell it writes, or should some (for example Schedule 1 lines 101, 104 and 403) still be traced to the books?
