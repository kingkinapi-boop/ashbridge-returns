## What the export says (cell ids; shapes only)

**"Imported" = exactly the Auto-fill cells.** All 75 are inside "Entered this year or last year". The 13 entered but not imported are what Add return wrote: tax year start and end (Ident120, Ident121), corporation's name (Ident311), BN (Ident7), the iFirm client code and integration cells (Ident451, 455, 456, 460, 461, 486, 487, 492) and `IFirm.ContactPartner`.

**Overlap with our import:** no confirmed id in `reference/sample-clients/lib/taxprep-cells.json` is Auto-filled. But the S8 rows (`CCACat.FD08C[n]`) and S1 lines 101, 104, 403 are cells our import will write once mapped, and `FD08C[n]` copy numbers collide: an import of `FD08C[1]` overwrites an Auto-filled class. Rule for the build: import after Auto-fill only into copies Auto-fill did not use, or never import S8 rows into a return with Auto-filled classes; RT-14 classes these cells "imported (CRA)", not orphans.

**CK-12 (opening balances from Auto-fill):** ERDTOH and NERDTOH: yes (closing balances of the selected year, so the opening of the next year). Opening losses, GRIP and the capital dividend account: not filled for this corporation (no S4, S53 or S89 cells). CK-12 keeps "not checked: no evidence" for those unless a future Auto-fill shows them.

**Which year to pull:** choosing the year's own assessment (2025 for a 2025 return) fills the current-year column with that year's assessed figures (closing UCC, closing RDTOH). For the real work, the return being prepared is the year after the last assessment, and Auto-fill's numbers are its openings. This test proved the shape; the year mapping is what the build must handle.
