Ashbridge T2 Design Review

Review of the production system design, 28 Sep 2026. Numbered links are checked sources. Everything else is judgment.

1Verdict

The spine is right: source at birth, a CSV round trip with diffs, and a full review with flags first.

But the round trip as written cannot prove what Taxprep holds, the ledger has no home for adjustments and judgment, and three rules break your own fixed decisions.

All of it can be fixed on paper before phase 1, and most fixes make the build smaller.

2Top 10 improvements

  1. Prove every round tripMedium

    ProblemOne compare at lock cannot tell a failed import from a preparer edit, or a rolled-forward value from a typed one. Taxprep numbers repeating rows by copy ([1], [2]) and does not import blank cells.1 The whole trace, and the learning loop, stand on this diff.

    Round trip in four exports. 0 Baseline: after roll-forward, before any import. 1 Receipt: right after import, with Taxprep's import report; every imported cell must be there with its value. 2 Lock. 3 Transmit. Each import carries a token in a cell that is never sent to CRA; every export must return the latest token, the business number and the year end. Repeating rows match by a natural key (CCA class, shareholder, slip), never by copy number. Re-imports send changed cells only. Export settings are fixed, and a file saved through Excel is refused.

  2. Add a transmit gateSmall

    Problem"Any later change voids approval" cannot work. The system only sees Taxprep through exports, so an unlock and edit after approval is invisible. The key control question is: is what we filed what the CPA approved?

    Before the T183CORP goes out, and again before transmit, ops uploads a fresh export. It must match the CPA-final fingerprint cell for cell. If not, the step is blocked and the changed cells go back to review.

  3. Add an adjustments layerLarge

    Problem"Figure = formula of facts" has no place for reclasses, accruals, allocations, estimates or corrections, which is most year-end work. Judgment hides inside formulas, and the CPA cannot see "client's books, what we changed, and why".

    Figures come from a working trial balance. Each account starts at the client's balance, or zero when there are no books. Every change is an adjusting entry that nets to zero, with a type (reclass, accrual, allocation, estimate, correction), a reason and its sources. Each account maps to one GIFI code. The trace shows the client balance, each entry, then the final figure. Bank-only files start from categorised bank lines, each sourced to its page and line. The balance sheet and retained earnings then tie by construction.

  4. Stop AI guessing sourcesMedium

    ProblemOrphans get an AI-proposed source. Rule 1 names this exact failure. A plausible wrong link is worse than none, because it looks sourced.

    Judgment inputs (CCA claims, dividend designations, elections, business limit sharing) are entered in our app with a reason, then imported. Typing in Taxprep is allowed only on an approved cell list. The preparer sources every orphan. AI may list documents that contain the exact value, but never pre-selects one.

  5. Split ties from reconciliationsMedium

    ProblemSeveral "hard ties" have normal differences. GST/HST line 101 includes exempt and zero-rated sales under the regular method, but includes the tax and excludes them under the quick method.45 Bank balances have outstanding items. T4 and T5 run by calendar year. With no materiality threshold, these will fire on nearly every file, and people learn to click past flags.

    A tie must agree to the dollar. A reconciliation shows both amounts and a list of reconciling items, each with a type (timing, exempt or zero-rated, outstanding items, calendar versus fiscal year) and a source. Only the unexplained remainder raises an exception. AI may propose items; code checks that they add up.

  6. Enforce the full reviewSmall

    ProblemNothing records that every part of the return was seen. Rule 7 should be a record, not a habit. A fixed order is also faster.

    Sections come in a fixed order: brief, balance sheet, income statement, Schedule 1, then each schedule present. A section is marked reviewed once shown and passed. Approve stays off until every section is marked. The approval stores the sections, the time on each and every source opened.

  7. Grade evidence by who made itSmall

    Problem"CRA data" counts as independent, but HST returns, remittances and T4 and T5 summaries are the client's own filings. Judgment figures have no marker. Red mixes an exception into a strength scale. A green dot on a client-reported number is false comfort.

    Each source has an origin: third party (bank, lender, OHIP, slips issued to the company, CRA's own records such as instalments credited and assessments), client filed with CRA, client prepared, client said, or judgment. Dot: green when a third party agrees, grey for one third-party source, amber for client only, purple for judgment. An open exception is a separate flag, not a dot colour.

  8. Make AI grounding a code checkMedium

    Problem"No ledger record, discard" throws away findings about missing items, which are the red team's best output. Nothing checks that an extracted number is really on the page.

    Every AI output is JSON with citations: a ledger record, a document box or a return cell. Code checks each one; a quoted number must appear in the OCR words inside its box. A failed citation drops the item and is counted against that step. A finding about a missing item cites where it should be. The red team uses a different model and reads the printed return and the raw documents, not our facts or notes. AI never clears an item.

  9. Fix learning-loop blameMedium

    ProblemThe table says any preparer change means "the AI got it wrong". Many are cells AI was not allowed to fill, facts it held back, fixes made in our app before the draft was saved, or late client information. Wrong causes build the wrong fixes, and the AI match rate will look better than it is.

    Each cell has an owner: AI-filled, held back, preparer-only, rolled forward or calculated. Only AI-filled cells count against AI. The AI's raw facts are saved before any person touches them, and AI accuracy is measured on those. Code rules set the cause first (late information, reading, mapping, judgment); AI tags only what is left, marked unconfirmed. Each week the top ten become draft build tasks, each with a failing test built from anonymised facts. A fixed test set runs before any model or prompt change.

  10. Pin the contracts firstMedium

    ProblemThe doc lists stages, but not states, transitions, what voids what, or data shapes. Coding agents fill gaps by inventing. OCR and AI jobs do not belong inside web requests.

    Before phase 1, approve: the return state machine (states, moves, who, what each move voids); the tables; the CSV contract with sample files; one JSON schema per AI step; and the check format (inputs, rule, tie or reconciliation, when it applies). OCR, AI and diffs run as queued jobs with retries and a status per file. Every output stores the model, prompt and mapping versions. Staff screens deploy apart from the client app and share only the database.

CCH's help says blank cells are not imported, and T2 year-start and year-end cells are ignored on import.12 That limits "any cell".Never rely on import to clear a value or set the year end. Test both in phase 0 and prove them by export.
"Older than the last change" cannot be checked. Taxprep changes are invisible to us.Tokens and exports 0 to 3 (item 1), plus the transmit gate.
AI writes the client summary and drafts client questions. Preparer free text also reaches the client. This breaks "no AI talks to clients" and "preparers never contact clients".Summary from a code template filled from CPA-final. Questions from an approved bank with slots, chosen per file.

4Cut or defer

  • The second AI that trims questions. Code already knows which facts are found.
  • The story check. It overlaps the checklist and the red team.
  • AI-drafted fixes for CPA comments.
  • The AI that challenges thin answers. A code rule (no source, or a banned phrase such as "per client") does it.
  • AI cause tagging and AI fix proposals. Start with code rules and a ranked list.
  • Our own map of Taxprep's calculations. Use the calculated-lines export.
  • Redrawing every schedule. Structure the GIFI and key schedules; show the rest as the printed return inside the coverage tracker.
  • PDF "file details" forensics. Keep the fingerprint and roll checks.
  • "Two AI passes that disagree". It is undefined; drop it until two passes exist.

5Missing

  • Tax pools: RDTOH (eligible and non-eligible), GRIP, CDA, losses and donations open at last year's assessed closing.
  • Schedule 1 ties: amortization add-back equals GIFI 8670; CCA equals Schedule 8; half of GIFI 8523 meals added back; tax penalties and interest added back.9
  • Specified investment business: flag property income when the company has five or fewer full-time employees.13
  • Foreign reporting: T1135 when foreign property cost tops $100,000 (from memory, not re-checked), plus T106 and T1134 triggers.
  • Slips the return implies: dividends paid need T5s, and accrued bonuses need T4s. Flag when CRA data shows none.
  • Associated group record: Schedule 23 shares must match on every group return and total no more than $500,000.
  • Due dates: filing at six months; balance due at two months, or three for qualifying CCPCs.11
  • Late evidence: a document after lock reopens only the facts it touches; it voids approval only if a linked figure changes.
  • After filing: an assessment that differs opens a work item. An amended return needs a new T183CORP.10
  • Ontario annual return: filed on the Ontario Business Registry since 2021, not with the T2.12 Add it to the after-filing steps, or list it as manual.
  • Locked records: the database refuses edits and deletes on events and versions. Keep the binder at least six years.10
  • Image redaction: black out SINs and account numbers on page images before any AI sees them, not only in text.
  • File ownership: one preparer holds a file at a time; roles for preparer, ops and CPA.

6Better alternatives

Our app is the only input surface

Every input, judgment included, is entered in our app and imported. Taxprep only calculates and files.

Gain: orphans nearly vanish, every value is sourced at birth, and the learning signal is clean.

Cost: an input screen for each judgment area, and an import and export loop each time the preparer wants Taxprep's result.

Review on the printed return

Put click targets on Taxprep's own printed PDF instead of redrawing schedules from CSV.

Gain: the CPA sees the real return, every schedule and calculated line, with nothing lost by our renderer.

Cost: a map from cells to page positions for each release, and repeated values can be ambiguous.

  • CRA: which Represent a Client screens show HST line 101, payroll totals, and T4 and T5 summary totals.
  • CPA Ontario or a practice adviser: when the firm assembles the financial statements, what engagement that is, and how Schedule 141 must be answered.
  • OCR and AI vendors: which endpoints keep data in Canada and keep nothing after the call.
