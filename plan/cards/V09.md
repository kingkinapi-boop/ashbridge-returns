## When it starts
This card waits until D08 (exceptions and orphans) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D08 done then.

## Goal
One page per return where the preparer answers every exception (fixed, explained with a source or reason, or accepted risk for the CPA), sees last year's accepted explanation offered and never applied by itself, resolves what the trace still lists with the cite action, and signs the return over to the CPA only when nothing remains.

## Build
- `src/modules/respond/` (`// @mutate` on each file; mutation 100 per marked file, ARC-15):
  - `readyForReview(returnId, deps)`: what still blocks: T12's `blockers` (called again, as T12's note requires) and every exception and every flag whose X00 `statusOf` is `open`; an `awaiting_cpa` exception does not block (it goes to the CPA). Each item names what resolves it.
  - `signForReview(returnId, actor, deps)`: the assigned preparer only (V00's access contract), refused for AI or system (AI-7) and while `readyForReview` is not empty (listing it); moves respond to review through F02. The guard `respond_to_review` calls `readyForReview` and is registered with F02's lifecycle in `src/pipeline/deps.ts`. V04's rework guard reuses `readyForReview`.
- `src/app/(staff)/prepare/exceptions/` (assigned preparer, CPA, owner; trace, respond and rework states), built to D08's approved pages:
  - The list holds open flags and exceptions in one EX-4 list (red first, then dollar effect; an MOJ sortable table when longer than 5 rows, default order EX-4), each with its check, amount, tax effect and status in words, and one answer box (RV-24, EX-1): radios Fixed, Explained, Accepted risk with none preselected and their reveals (a source pointer through the source viewer, or a reason). X00's refusal (EX-2: no source or reason, "per client" and the like) shows as a GOV.UK error message at the field naming the rule.
  - Last year's answer (EX-3): where `proposeFromLastYear` returns one, it is shown with its year and source and a "Use last year's answer" button; nothing is answered until a person presses it, and a stale source is refused with the reason.
  - Orphans, overrides, dropped cells and cells changed outside the trace (RV-24, RT-16): V12's rows and cite action, imported from `src/app/(staff)/prepare/cite/`, never copied; candidates listed with none selected.
  - "Sign and send to review" only when `readyForReview` is empty; otherwise what remains as links (rule 8). Each answer runs in place, keeps the scroll and moves focus to the next row (rule 19).
- Shortcuts through U01: next row, answer, open source, save.
