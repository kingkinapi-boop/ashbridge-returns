## When it starts
This card waits until D12 (the cite button and orphan list) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D12 done then.

## Goal
After the lock export is traced, the preparer sees every value typed in Taxprep that needs a source (orphans, including every tax choice) and every override and dropped cell that needs a reason, and resolves each in one step beside it, so the return can be signed off.

## Build
- `src/app/(staff)/prepare/cite/`: built to D12's approved design, a list per return (an MOJ sortable table when longer than 5 rows, default order by form order then identifier, stated in D12's brief) of T12's `blockers` that a person resolves here: orphans (tax choices marked with their kind: CCA claim, dividend designation, election, business limit share, loss or donation claim), overrides (imported and typed values side by side), dropped cells, and "changed outside the trace" cells. Each row shows the form, line or GIFI code, Taxprep's description and the value.
- The cite button beside each orphan and tax choice (RV-22): one step records either a source from T12's `candidateSources` (none pre-selected; each candidate opens in the source viewer, V01) or another source pointer, or a written reason; the row then shows its source and dot. Overrides and dropped cells take a written reason (or, for a dropped cell, "re-import" which links back to V06).
- `allowed` orphans (RT-15) are listed apart, collapsed, needing nothing.
- A count of what remains, and a link back to the checklist's sign-off step (V06) when nothing remains.
- Server actions through the data-access layer (SEC-2) call T12 only; no AI suggestion is shown as a choice (AI-7).
- Shortcuts through U01: next row, cite, open candidate, save.

## Test fixtures (spec-writer)
- A test-world return in trace with T12's fixture blockers (an orphan with two exact-value candidates and one that rounds, a typed CCA claim, an override, a dropped cell, a "changed outside the trace" cell, seven `allowed` orphans); a return assigned to another preparer.

## Golden files (spec-writer)
- The ARIA snapshot of the list before and after resolving every row (`e2e/staff/__golden__/`).

## Acceptance checks
1. RV-22, RV-24: every blocker from T12 is listed once with its class, value and description; tax choices show their kind; `allowed` orphans are apart and need nothing.
2. RT-16: the candidates are listed with none selected; choosing one and saving records the cite in one step and the row shows the source and its dot; the rounding candidate is listed apart; AI-7: no AI suggestion is shown as a choice and the page has no action that resolves a row without a person's click.
3. TB-6: the typed CCA claim is cited with a written reason in one step; an empty reason shows an error summary and records nothing.
4. RT-14: the override and the dropped cell each take a written reason; the dropped cell's "re-import" leads to V06's import step.
5. RT-18: when the last row is resolved the page says nothing remains and links to sign-off; the count matches T12's `blockers`.
6. SEC-2: a preparer cannot see or post to another preparer's return.
7. RV-53: the list matches D12's approved pages within the visual threshold, and D12's click and page-load budgets for "cite one orphan" hold.
8. RV-54: U02's sweeps pass; the whole task works by keyboard alone; the golden ARIA snapshots match.
