## Goal
When ops sends the T183CORP (in CCH Digital Signature, outside Returns), Returns records it against the standing approval and writes the approval summary the client app shows: the six numbers the CPA approved against last year, and one assumption row per unanswered question, as ids and numbers only. The signed certificate ops uploads moves the return to ready_to_file. A void withdraws the summary, and a new approval needs a new T183CORP. No sentence is written anywhere; the client's wording lives in the client app.

## Spec
- Fixtures (pinned clock): C01 approved with last year's values (B03); C05 approved with no prior year; a return approved with two sent questions left unanswered; a certificate PDF made with W20's framework naming the corporation "(Test)" and the year end, one naming another corporation, a non-PDF file; users: ops, the CPA, the owner, a preparer, an AI actor.
- Classes:
  - Approval summary values: the six rows (`net_income`, `taxable_income`, `federal_tax`, `ontario_tax`, `instalments`, `balance_or_refund`) carry, in integer cents, the values of the review-line cells in the standing approval's fingerprint (T07 meaning keys mapped by `data/clientsign/summary-lines.json`, the same mapping V02 shows), never a later export's value; `prior_value_cents` is B03's last-year value, or null with no prior year (C05). Property (fast-check, fixed seed): for any approved review-line values, each row's value equals the fingerprinted cell's value.
  - Assumption rows: one `assumption` row per question of the sent question list with no answer at the time of sending, its slots naming the question's bank item id and fact id; none when every question is answered.
  - END-7, ARC-2: every row written passes `BridgeHandoffRowSchema` and the table's checks; a property over the writer's inputs finds no row with a text value outside ids, numbers and slot values; the module imports nothing from `src/contracts/ai.ts` (source scan through `readOwnSource`, with a sentinel).
  - RV-30, FLOW-2 (approved to client_sign): `recordT183Sent` stores who, when and the approval id, writes the summary as a new `list_version` with status `sent`, and moves the return through F02 in one transaction; guard `approved_to_client_sign` passes only with a T183CORP sent record for the standing approval and its summary written; each refusal names what is missing.
  - RV-30, FLOW-2 (client_sign to ready_to_file): `receiveT183Certificate` accepts a PDF only, stores it write-once and fingerprinted (A05, EV-2), links it to the T183CORP sent record, and moves the return; guard `client_sign_to_ready_to_file` passes only with a certificate linked to the sent record for the standing approval. A certificate whose text names another corporation is refused naming it; one where neither the name nor the year end is found is accepted with the flag "certificate not checked" for a person, never a silent pass.
  - FLOW-5: a void of the approval (through T08 and the pipeline) moves every `sent` approval row for that approval to `withdrawn`; a T183CORP record or certificate for a voided approval no longer satisfies either guard; after re-approval a new T183CORP and a new summary version are required.
  - FLOW-8: an amended return (a new cycle, so a new approval) is not moved by the earlier cycle's T183CORP or certificate.
  - SEC-8: `t183RetainUntil` is six years after the filing date in T09's filing record (calendar years; a 29 Feb filing date gives 28 Feb), and "until filed plus six years" (no date yet) before filing; nothing in the module deletes a record or a file (no delete method exists; the file store refuses delete).
  - Who (AI-7, SEC-2): a preparer, an AI actor or the system recording a T183CORP or uploading a certificate is refused with the reason and nothing is stored; either call in a state other than its own is refused naming the state.
  - SEC-7: UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on the tables this card adds are refused (db test on PGlite and Postgres 16); approval rows in `client_handoff` change only their status and sent time (F07's trigger).
- Golden file (`src/modules/clientsign/__golden__/`): C01's approval summary rows.
