## Goal
Every value a return carries from last year comes from the best source there is, in a fixed order: last year's CPA-final and assessed version when we filed it; otherwise last year's T2 PDF and its notice of assessment; and for the CRA balances, the latest assessed figure from the CRA data capture. A value taken from a return someone else prepared (a converted value) stays amber until it ties to the assessment or to our own filed version. Where no source exists, the result says so, and the opening check is "not checked: no evidence", never a pass.

## Build
- `src/modules/books/prior/`:
  - `priorYearValues(returnId)`: for each prior-year item (last year's closing GIFI balance sheet lines and 3849 retained earnings, non-capital and capital losses by year, RDTOH eligible and non-eligible, GRIP, capital dividend account, UCC by class, donations carried forward, instalments, and the six brief numbers RV-2 compares against), the value, its source pointer and why that source was chosen:
    1. Our own last-year version marked CPA-final and assessed (read through the versions contract when it exists; until phase 2 builds versions, tests insert one through F01's tables).
    2. Otherwise last year's T2 PDF facts (E17), and the notice of assessment facts (E18) where the assessment states the item.
    3. For losses, RDTOH, GRIP and the capital dividend account (CK-12): the latest CRA assessed figure from the CRA data capture facts (E19), stored with its pull date; UCC by class and donations carried forward come from last year's filed closing, and a reassessment of that year on file is flagged.
    4. None of these: "no evidence", with the list of what was looked for.
  - Converted values (TB-8): a value from last year's T2 PDF prepared by another firm (K02) is stored as a fact with origin client prepared, so L01 gives it an amber dot; when the notice of assessment or the CRA capture states the same item and the two agree to the dollar under F05's rule, an "agrees with" link is written through L01's rule `prior-return-agrees-assessment` (this card adds the rule to `data/ledger/agree-rules.json`, with TB-8 as its source), and the value's source becomes last year's assessed return (grey). A disagreement is flagged for a person with both amounts and both pointers, and the converted value stays amber.
  - Opening retained earnings (TB-5, extending B05): when last year's filed GIFI 3849 is available from step 1 or 2, it is compared with the year's opening retained earnings from QBO; a difference refuses the books with both amounts and both pointers, the same refusal B05 makes. Where B05 said "opening not checked: no evidence" and this source exists, the check now runs.
  - Amalgamation or wind-up (CK-12): the client app holds no such field (`reference/onboarding-contract.md` sections 1 and 2, client-app commit f87a0043), so it is a fact a person enters with a source. `recordReorganisation(returnId, { kind, effectiveDate }, actor, reason)` (kind `none`, `amalgamation` or `wind-up`) proposes E03's facts `corporation.reorganisation.kind` and `corporation.reorganisation.effective_date` through L00's `proposeFact`, with a reason-a-person-wrote pointer (EV-5; L01 gives it a purple dot). When the current fact says amalgamation or wind-up, no prior-year value is carried automatically and one flag for a person names the fact, its author and its reason. With no such fact, or `none`, prior-year values carry as above; nothing is assumed either way. The screen that calls it comes with the return record (a later card); until then tests and journeys call the function.
  - Every result names the source order step used, carries this card's code release and the versions of the facts it read (ARC-10).
- `src/modules/books/index.ts` gains the prior-year exports.
- `src/pipeline/steps/books.ts` (B05's step) runs `priorYearValues` and the 3849 opening check after `readReturnBooks`, and reports them with the books result.

## Acceptance checks
1. TB-8: for C01, every prior-year item comes from our own CPA-final and assessed version (step 1), equal to the golden file; for C02, from the T2 PDF and assessment facts (step 2), equal to its golden file.
2. TB-8, EV-11: the K02-style converted loss that disagrees with the assessment by $500.00 is flagged with both amounts and pointers and its dot stays amber (through L01's `dotOf`); the values that agree get an agrees-with link and a grey dot.
3. CK-12: losses, RDTOH, GRIP and the capital dividend account come from the CRA capture with its pull date when one exists, over the T2 PDF; UCC by class and donations come from last year's filed closing; with the reassessment on file, those items are flagged; with no source at all, the item says "not checked: no evidence" and lists what was looked for.
4. TB-5: last year's filed 3849 that differs from the year's opening retained earnings in QBO refuses the books with both amounts; when they agree, nothing is refused; for C09 (first year) every prior-year item is "no prior year" and nothing is refused.
5. TB-8, CK-12: the amalgamation return carries no prior-year value automatically and has one flag for a person naming the fact, its author and reason; the fact's pointer is the person's reason and its dot is purple (L01's `dotOf`); after `none` is recorded, the values carry and the flag is gone; `recordReorganisation` with no reason or an unknown kind is refused with the reason.
6. TB-8: every result names its source step and carries version stamps; property (fast-check, fixed seed): for any set of available sources, the chosen source is always the first available in the fixed order.
7. TB-5: the books step for C02 through the pipeline reports the prior-year values and the opening check with the books result.
