## Goal
After intake, each document is cut into the separate documents it holds, each piece gets a kind, a period and an entity by written rules, a document for the wrong year or company is flagged and not used, and signs of tampering mark it suspect. Code decides; where code cannot, the document is flagged for a person, never guessed and never sent to AI here.

## Build
- `data/documents/kinds.json`: one entry per document kind the extractor families read (bank, card and lender statements, slips issued to the company, T4 and T5 summaries, HST returns, payroll remittances, CRA notices and assessments, invoices, trial balances, financial statements, last year's T2 and notice of assessment, OHIP remittance advice): the words and layout signs that identify it (title phrases, issuer names, form numbers), the words that begin a new document of the kind ("Page 1 of", a statement title, a form header), where its period and entity sit, and its origin key for L01 (EV-10). Data, not code: a new kind is a new entry.
- `src/modules/documents/classify/`:
  - `split(document)` (EV-3): from the page words, finds where a new document starts (a first-page sign, a restart of "Page 1 of N", a change of kind, issuer or period) and writes one child document per piece, each pointing to its page range in the parent; the parent is kept. A one-piece PDF gets no children.
  - `classify(piece)`: the kind whose signs match, with the matched words as the reason. The client's upload slot (`document_kind` from the bridge) is a hint: when it disagrees with the matched kind, or no kind or more than one matches, the piece gets kind `unknown` and a flag for a person, with the reason.
  - Period and entity: the statement or slip period and the company name and business number from the words in the places `kinds.json` names. A period outside the return's tax year (with the kind's allowed overlap, for example a statement running past year end) or an entity that does not match the return's corporation is flagged "wrong year" or "wrong entity" and marked not used (EV-3); nothing downstream reads a not-used piece.
  - Tamper signs (EV-4): the fingerprint is already E00's; here, a PDF saved again after creation (more than one end-of-file marker, or a modification date after the creation date), and for statement kinds the roll check: opening balance plus the activity equals the closing balance, in integer cents (F05's arithmetic), page by page and for the whole statement (EV-7). A failed sign marks the piece `suspect` with the reason; E01 marks every fact from a suspect piece suspect.
  - Every result carries the rules version (a hash of `kinds.json`) and this card's code release (ARC-10).
- `src/pipeline/steps/read.ts`: replaces F10's read step. Reading itself now happens at intake (E00, ARC-11), so this step takes the stored reading results and runs split, classify, period and entity, and tamper signs, returning the pieces with their kinds and marks; it never runs a reading engine again.
- `db/schema/10_documents.sql`: child documents and page ranges, kind, period, entity, the used and suspect marks with reasons, and the flags for a person. Append-only where F01's rule says so: a new classification is a new row with an event.

## Acceptance checks
1. EV-3: the combined PDF becomes three pieces with the expected page ranges and kinds; the parent is kept; a one-statement PDF gets no children.
2. EV-3: the prior-year statement is flagged "wrong year" and the other company's statement "wrong entity", both marked not used, each with the matched words as the reason.
3. EV-4, EV-7: the statement one cent off fails the roll check and is marked suspect with the page and the difference in cents; property (fast-check, fixed seed): for any opening balance and list of activity in cents, the roll check passes exactly when the closing balance equals their sum.
4. EV-4: the re-saved statement is marked suspect with the reason; the clean statements are not (no false alarm on any C01 or C10 fixture).
5. EV-3: a page no kind matches, and the T5 summary uploaded under the bank-statement slot, get kind `unknown` and a flag for a person with the reason; neither is guessed.
6. EV-10: the kind ids in `kinds.json` are exactly E03's `DOCUMENT_KINDS` (none missing, none extra), and every kind has an origin key that is one of EV-10's five origins or last year's assessed return (L01 reads the same keys).
7. ARC-10: every classification row carries the rules version and code release; changing `kinds.json` changes the rules version.
8. EV-3: the read step for the combined PDF through the pipeline returns its three pieces with kinds and marks, and a spy on the reading contract shows no second read.
9. EV-3: the walking skeleton's C01 fixture page classifies to a kind in `kinds.json` (not `unknown`), and SK0's checks still pass through the pipeline (cloud).
