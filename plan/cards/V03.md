## When it starts
This card waits until D02 (the CPA review) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D02 done then.

## Goal
The full return after the brief, every section in RV-1's fixed order, each number clickable with its dot, last year and the change, opening the three panes; each section marked only by "Reviewed, next", the mark coming off when a number in it changes; a coverage tracker; the CPA's judgment on each accepted-risk exception; and Approve only when every section is marked, storing the review record through T08.

## Build
- `data/review/form-sections.json` (data before code): each Taxprep form in the trial release mapped to one of T08's RV-1 section ids, its view (`cells` or `printed`, RV-9) and, for `printed`, the header words that find its pages in T07's printed return. A form in a lock export that the file does not name goes to a section "Forms not yet placed" after the last section, still needing a mark, and is named on its section and in `readyToApprove` ('form not placed: <form>'), and the review passed to T08 names it (never skipped silently).
- `src/modules/review/marks/` (`// @mutate` on each file; mutation 100 per marked file, ARC-15):
  - `markSection(returnId, sectionId, cpa)`: CPA role only (V00's access contract), refused for AI or system actors (AI-7); stores the mark with the section's cell values (identifier, natural row key, value) at that moment.
  - `marksOf(returnId)`: per section `marked` or `unmarked`; a mark comes off when any cell value in that section in the latest accepted lock export differs from the values stored with it, compared by natural row key, never copy number (RV-5, LL-3). `sectionChanges(returnId)` returns each changed cell with before and after (V04 reads it).
  - `recordView(returnId, sectionId, cpa, at)` and `secondsOn(returnId)`: time per section from view events on the injected clock; a gap over the idle limit (firm parameter, start 5 minutes) is not counted.
  - `judgeRisk(exceptionId, verdict, cpa)` (EX-1): the CPA's judgment on an accepted-risk answer, `accepted` with a reason; the `comment` verdict is written by V04 with its comment id; refused for anyone but the CPA and for AI.
  - `readyToApprove(returnId)`: the unmarked sections and the unjudged accepted risks, each as a link target; empty means Approve may show.
- `db/schema/74_review.sql`: marks, section views and risk judgments, append-only with F01's `refuse_change` triggers, row-level security on, CHECK lists on verdicts.
- `src/app/(staff)/review/sections/`, shown to every role V00's `canSee` lets see the return (the same record tabs for everyone: decision 0020 Z20-6, A387): the CPA marks, judges and approves; the owner sees all; the assigned preparer sees the sections read-only, with no mark, judge, approve or comment control in the page or its server actions; the CPA's comments stay hidden from the preparer until `sendForRework` (V04; a draft is not sent); every source open is logged (SEC-3). Who sees a return is V00's rule, unchanged here. Built to D02's approved pages, extending V14's components (V14's early route and its journey are folded in here and retired):
  - One table per section in RV-1's order, never sortable (rule 6); every number a link with its own address, its dot in words, last year and the change (RV-3); flagged numbers highlighted where they sit, more by tier through X01's `highlightsFor`, never fewer sections (RV-8). `printed` forms show their printed pages and still need a mark (RV-9). A section with no cells in this return shows 'nothing in this return for this section' and still needs a mark (RULE-7).
  - The three panes for every number (RV-4): V14's trace extended to Taxprep cells (T04 class, cite or reason, `sourcesOf`), the source in V01; every open goes through `logSourceOpen` (SEC-3).
  - The MOJ side navigation as the coverage tracker, marked or unmarked in words; "Reviewed, next" marks and moves on, never unmarks (rule 22).
  - The pane action bar lives in `actions.tsx` in this folder (V04 and V13 add to it).
  - Approve (RV-10): the Approve link shows only when `readyToApprove` is empty; until then the page lists what remains as links. The Approve page (MOJ confirm with a summary list, double-submit prevention) calls T08's `approve` with every mark, every risk judgment, the seconds per section and the source-log ids of this review.
- Shortcuts through U01 (RV-6): next flag, next number, open source, reviewed and next section, approve (moves focus to the control only).

## Spec
- Fixtures: C01 in review with a later lock export changing one cell in Schedule 1 after it was marked; a return holding a form not in the data file; a section with no cells; an accepted-risk exception; the largest test-world return; users: the CPA, the owner, the assigned preparer, preparer B, ops.
- Classes: RV-1 (every section in the fixed order, golden ARIA snapshot); RV-3 and RV-8 (dots in words, highlights per tier, the full section list for every tier); RV-4 (three panes; under 1000 ms click to source paint on the largest return, `performance.mark`); RV-5 (mark only by "Reviewed, next"; the changed cell takes the Schedule 1 mark off and no other; a cell added to or removed from the section (a new repeating-group copy, a cell emptied) takes the mark off; copies renumbered with equal values do not (LL-3, natural row key); property over cell edits with a fixed seed); RV-9 (a printed form shows its pages and blocks Approve until marked; an unplaced form is named on its section and in `readyToApprove` as 'form not placed: <form>', reaches T08 in the review, and needs a mark; a section with no cells shows 'nothing in this return for this section' and needs a mark); RV-10 (no Approve link while a section or an accepted risk is open; the list links each); RV-11 (T08 receives every mark, every risk judgment, seconds and source id; idle gaps not counted on the pinned clock); EX-1 and AI-7 (a preparer, ops, AI and system actor cannot mark, judge or approve); SEC-7 (UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE refused on the new tables); SEC-2, written from V00's access table (the CPA and the owner see every return's sections; the assigned preparer sees them read-only, and no mark, judge, approve or comment control is in the page or answers its server actions for her; preparer B and ops, whom V00 does not let see a return in review, get "not found", the same as an address that does not exist); SEC-3 (every source open by any role writes one source-log row); RV-53 (matches D02 within the visual threshold; D02's budgets for "review one section" and "approve"); RV-54 (U02's sweeps; keyboard alone; focus never hidden by a pane).
