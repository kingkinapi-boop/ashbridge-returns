# D00 Design basis: GOV.UK and MOJ components, Ashbridge brand

Spec commit: (spec-writer fills)

## Goal
The one place every design page and every screen takes its look from, so the staff app is GOV.UK from the first line and reads as the same firm as the client app.

## Build
- `design/basis/settings.scss`: GOV.UK Frontend 6.x settings for the Ashbridge brand: `$govuk-font-family` (Roboto), `$govuk-functional-colours` from the colour map in `reference/design-basis.md` (never the client's two failing colours), the wide container (up to 1600 px) for tables and review screens.
- `design/basis/build.mjs`: a small Node script that compiles the stylesheet with Dart Sass from the official `govuk-frontend` and `@ministryofjustice/frontend` packages, and renders design pages from their official Nunjucks macros with made-up test-world data.
- `design/basis/parts.md`: every GOV.UK and MOJ part the designs may use, with its MOJ status (Official, To be reviewed, Experimental), and every `app-` exception with its reason (the three-pane review, the coverage tracker, keyboard shortcuts).
- The Ashbridge Tax logo copied from the client app (`public/brand/ashbridge-tax-logo.png`, read-only source) into `design/basis/`.
- A sample page showing every part in the brand, for the review pack.

## Acceptance checks
1. RV-55: the compiled stylesheet uses Roboto and the brand colours, and contains no GOV.UK crown, logotype or GDS Transport.
2. RV-54: every colour in the settings meets WCAG AA contrast for its use (text 4.5:1, borders and focus 3:1), computed in a test.
3. RV-54: axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) finds no violation on the sample page.
4. RV-52: every class on the sample page is `govuk-`, `moj-` or a listed `app-` class.
5. RV-52: the build script runs in under a minute with no network access after `npm ci`.

## Done means approved
The checks above make it ready for Zo. D00 is done only when Zo approves the sample page at a design sitting (RV-53), and the Lead marks it done then, as the design family does for D02 to D13. U00, which every screen is built from, waits on that.
