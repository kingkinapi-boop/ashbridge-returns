## When it starts
This card waits until D02 and D08 are approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks them done then.

## Goal
For a simple comment (a presentation comment, or an error comment on one number; A34), AI drafts the fix with citations. Code checks every citation and that the draft stays on the commented number. Nothing changes until the assigned preparer approves the draft, and then the change goes through the normal round trip (RT-8). AI never approves, responds to, resolves or closes anything.

## Build
- `src/contracts/ai.ts`: one new step type `fix_draft`: `answer` with `change` (exactly one of `fact_value` with fact id and new value, or `taxprep_entry` with identifier and value), a short `explanation` and at least one evidence citation; or AI-6's `cannot_tell`.
- `src/modules/ai/fixes/` (`// @mutate` on `simple.ts`, `scope.ts` and `approve.ts`; mutation 100 per marked file, ARC-15):
  - `simple.ts`: `isSimple(comment)` in code: type presentation, or type error on exactly one cell; question and missing evidence never.
  - `job.ts`: the `ai:fix` job for one simple comment through I00's `groundedStep` (redaction, schema, citation checks, AI-10 stamps), inputs read-only: the comment, its cell, the figure's `sourcesOf` and their facts. Document text is data (AI-8). `src/pipeline/steps/fix-drafts.ts` queues one job per simple comment when a return moves review to rework; the handler is registered in `src/pipeline/handlers.ts` (one line).
  - `scope.ts`: code checks beyond I00's: a `fact_value` names a fact in the commented figure's `sourcesOf`, and its new value appears inside a cited document box or ledger record (I00's quoted-number check); a `taxprep_entry` names the commented cell's identifier. A draft that fails is dropped with its reason and counted against the step; the comment then shows no draft.
  - `approve.ts`: `approveDraft(draftId, preparer)`: the assigned preparer only (V00's access contract); refused for AI, system, the CPA, and a draft whose sources changed since it was made. A `fact_value` calls L00's `changeFact` (a new version, why naming the comment and draft) then `verifyFact` by that preparer, in one transaction, so T01's next re-import carries only that cell (RT-8). A `taxprep_entry` records the preparer's to-do on the comment; nothing in Returns changes and the next trace (T04, V12) checks it. `rejectDraft(draftId, preparer, reason)`. Neither responds to nor resolves the comment (V04).
- `db/schema/76_fixes.sql`: drafts (comment, output, stamps, kept and dropped items) and decisions, append-only with F01's `refuse_change` triggers, row-level security on, CHECK lists on kind and decision.
- `src/app/(staff)/review/fixes/`: the draft fix panel, mounted in V04's `comment-card.tsx`, built to D08's approved pages: before and after, the explanation, each citation opening the source viewer (V01), "Approve draft" and "Reject" with a reason; a `cannot_tell` draft shows its reason. Tagged "AI draft" in words.

## Spec
- Fixtures: C01 in rework with an error comment on a GIFI line fed by one fact whose document box holds the right value, a presentation comment on a Taxprep typed cell, a question comment, and recorded answers (A04's `recorded` engine under `__recordings__/`) including one citing a box that lacks the value, one naming a fact outside the figure's sources, one `cannot_tell`, and a document with a planted instruction.
- Classes: RV-12 (a table over all four comment types: presentation and error get a job, question and missing evidence never; the question fixture gets none); a draft whose sources changed since it was made is refused on approval, naming the change, and nothing is written; AI-1 and AI-4 (a draft with no citation, or a citation whose value is not in its box, is dropped and counted); scope (the off-figure fact is dropped); AI-6 (`cannot_tell` shows its reason, nothing else); AI-7 (an AI or system actor, the CPA, and preparer B cannot approve; before approval no fact, figure, import or comment row changes, checked by table counts); RT-8 and EV-8 (approving the `fact_value` writes one fact version verified by the preparer, and the next re-import holds only that cell, golden file); AI-8 (the planted instruction has no effect); AI-10 (each draft stores its stamps); AI-11 (with the `fix_draft` triple absent from `approved.json` the project engine is refused and no draft shows; the set holds one case per recorded answer above); SEC-7 (UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE refused on the new tables); SEC-2; RV-53 (matches D08 within the visual threshold; budget for "approve a draft fix"); RV-54 (U02's sweeps; keyboard alone).
