## When it starts
This card waits until D02 (the CPA review) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D02 done then.

## Goal
Phase 1's proof that the CPA review works: for one test-world return, the statements and GIFI section (balance sheet, income statement, retained earnings) in RV-1's order, every number clickable with its dot and last year's value and change, and a click opens the three panes (the return, the trace, the source) with the source painted in under a second (END-3). The full review with every section, marks and Approve is V03's; this slice is the part phase 1's data can fill, built to D02's approved design so V03 extends it rather than replacing it.

## Build
- `src/app/(staff)/review/early/` (the CPA review tab of the return record shell, V00; CPA and owner roles only, through V00's access rules), built to D02's approved design:
  - The left pane: the statements and GIFI sections in RV-1's order (balance sheet, income statement, retained earnings), each GIFI line from B01's `gifiTotals` with its code and name, this year's amount, last year's amount from B03 (or "no prior year" with its reason) and the change, and its dot from L01's `dotOfFigure` in words and colour (EV-11, rule 7). An open exception shows as a separate red flag, never as a dot colour (EV-12). Only these sections are shown, and the section list names only them (no placeholder for later sections, rule 16).
  - Every number is a button (RV-3). Clicking opens the middle pane, the trace (TB-7): how the line is built (its mapped accounts from B01, each with its QBO balance from B05's `accountTrace`, each adjusting entry with its type and reason and explained state, the final amount), each source with its dot, what agrees with it (L01's links), last year's value; and the right pane, the source viewer (V01) at the first source, with next and previous through all of them (TB-9).
  - The panes sit side by side at both rule 18 sizes; the source can open in the second window (V01).
  - No "Reviewed" marks, no comments and no Approve here (V03, V04): the slice adds no control those cards would have to remove.
- Timing (RV-4, END-3): the click and the source paint are marked with `performance.mark`; trace and source data for the section are loaded with the page so a click needs no extra round trip for the trace.
- Shortcuts through U01: next number, open source, next source.

## Acceptance checks
1. RV-1: the sections appear in RV-1's order (balance sheet, income statement, retained earnings) and the GIFI lines in code order within each; the golden ARIA snapshot matches.
2. RV-3, EV-11: every number is clickable and carries its dot in words; each line shows last year's value and the change; the line with an open exception shows a red flag beside a dot that is not red (EV-12).
3. TB-7: clicking the line built from three accounts shows in the trace each account's QBO balance with its snapshot pointer, the adjusting entry with its type and reason, and the final amount equal to the sum in cents.
4. RV-4: the click opens the trace in the middle and the source on the right at the first source with its box shown; next and previous step through every source in order.
5. RV-4, END-3 timing (cloud, production build): on the largest test-world return, from click to source paint is under 1000 ms, measured by `performance.mark`.
6. SEC-2 through V00: a preparer cannot open the CPA review (answers "not found").
7. RV-53: the slice matches D02's approved pages for the statements section within the visual threshold, and D02's budgets for "open a number's source" hold.
8. RV-54: U02's sweeps pass; every number, the panes and next and previous work by keyboard alone with visible focus never hidden by a pane.
