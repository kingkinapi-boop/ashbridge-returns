## Goal
The amount grammar is written down once, as a table, and used everywhere: reading one amount from text (`normaliseAmount`), reading the amount groups among a box's words (`amountGroups`, which `valueInBox` uses), and the formats the test-world renderer prints and the spreadsheet reader compares. F09's two rounds found the grammar spread over loose regexes that glued columns together and split signs from their amounts; this card replaces them with one lexer, one table and one group function.

## Defect note (known false positives on main until this card lands)
F09 lands with its round 2 grammar, which has these known faults (`reports/findings-F09-r2.md` findings 1 to 3 and RC-A). Nothing consumes amounts until this card lands (every consumer depends on F09A), so they are recorded here, not fixed in F09:
- A trailing "-" is taken greedily with no look-ahead, so "100.00" "-" "50.00" loses the second amount's sign.
- Joins reuse one-or-two-digit decimals: "1" ".5", "1" "234.5" and "1" ",234.5" join.
- `normaliseAmount` strips every space: "12 34" reads 1234.
- Siblings: a stray ")" spoils the amount before it; U+2212 is not a minus; en and em dashes have no rule; joins ignore the gap between words, so columns join; two bare integers join as space thousands.

## The grammar (the table; amber A296)
Tokens:
| Token | Pattern | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NUM | `0\|[1-9]\d{0,2}(,\d{3})+\|[1-9]\d*`, then optional `.d` or `.dd`, as a whole word | one-digit decimals only inside a single word |
| GRP3 | `\d{3}(\.\d\d)?` | a space-separated thousands group |
| CGRP | `,\d{3}(\.\d\d)?` | a comma thousands group as its own word |
| DEC2 | `\.\d\d` | decimals as their own word: exactly two digits |
| LEAD | `-` `−` (U+2212) `$` `(` `$-` `-$` `$(` `($` `−$` | leading marks |
| TRAIL | `-` `)` `CR` `DR` | trailing marks |
| SEP | anything else, including en and em dashes | ends a group |

- A group is `LEAD* NUM (GRP3|CGRP)* DEC2? TRAIL?`.
- Continuations (GRP3, CGRP, DEC2) join only before any decimals, and a joined decimal has exactly two digits.
- One sign mark per group; brackets balance (an unbalanced "(" or a stray ")" makes no group with that bracket); CR never stacks with a minus.
- Joins need the same line, consecutive reading order, and a gap between words no wider than the word height (the tolerance is a parameter with that default).
- Not a number: three decimals, "1.234,56", "1,23,456", leading zeros ("001234"; "0.56" is fine).
- **Dash rule (A296):** leading binds first. A "-" followed within the gap by an amount is that amount's leading sign; otherwise it is a trailing sign on the amount to its left. "100.00" "-" "50.00" finds 100.00 and -50.00.
- An amount of zero is 0, never -0, whatever its sign mark.

## Build
- `src/contracts/amount-grammar.ts` (carries `// @mutate`): one lexer (word to token), the table above as exported data, one group function over a list of words with positions, and:
  - `normaliseAmount(text)`: splits the text on spaces into words and must give exactly one group, else "not a number" with the reason; returns integer cents; no floating point on the way.
  - `amountGroups(words)`: the maximal amount groups among words (same line, reading order, the gap rule), each with its cents and the words it covers.
  - `AMOUNT_FORMATS`: the exported amount-format table (dollar sign or not, thousands commas or spaces, two decimals, negatives as leading minus, trailing minus, brackets, CR or DR) and `formatAmount(cents, format)`. W20 prints from it and A07 compares against it; nobody else keeps a format list.
  - `normaliseAmount`, `amountGroups` and `formatAmount` carry `@money` in their JSDoc (SC's rule R26 finds them by it; renumbered from R19, findings wave 2 fix 1g).
- `src/contracts/reading.ts`: only where it calls the grammar. `normaliseAmount` is re-exported from the grammar file (same name and result type, so F09's tests and the cards that read F09 keep working) and `valueInBox` compares whole groups from `amountGroups`. The reading schemas become strict at every depth at runtime (`BoxSchema`, `WordSchema`, `PageSchema`, `EngineStampSchema`, `ReadingResultSchema`: an unknown key is refused, never dropped; findings wave 2, RC2, fix 1b), and the A333 defects below are fixed. Nothing else in reading.ts changes.
- F09's existing acceptance tests stay green, except the grammar parts of checks 8 and 9, which this card's spec may replace by checks 14 to 16 (the spec says which tests it replaces and why).
