## Spec
- A check released with a note starting "wait:" is not offered again until the Lead reopens it (`update <card> check reopened --worker lead`), like specs and builds today. Planted: a "wait:" check, a second worker calls `next`, gets NOTHING or another job.
- A plain release (no "wait:") is still re-offered once, then held "needs Lead" (unchanged).
- A Lead reopen of a spec or build names the worker that may take it: `update <card> <role> reopened --worker lead --for <name>` lets that worker take it with `update <card> <role> working --worker <name>`; without `--for`, only `next` hands it out (the SC12 refusal of 3 Oct).
- `metrics.mjs`: `check_fails` counts "failed <card> check" lines (and keeps reading old "failed <card> build" lines); `jobs` counts claims (one per start of a spec, build or check, refits and releases included), not heartbeats. Planted: a claims history with 2 failed checks and 40 beats gives check_fails 2 and the true job count.
- From CQ8's build (A473): a test that a worker named `local-*` is never offered a job on a card whose Where line says only cloud, and next.mjs tags it "cloud only" (CQ8 built rule 3 without a spec test); the Where-line reader moves into `tools/lib.mjs`, one copy for claim.mjs and next.mjs.
