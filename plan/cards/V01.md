## When it starts
This card waits until D03 (the source viewer) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D03 done then.

## Goal
The one place any source opens (rule 20): beside the work, full height, at the right page with the figure boxed and scrolled into view, with next and previous when several sources feed a number, and in a second window that follows every click for a person with two monitors. It opens in well under a second, never shows a SIN or banking number, and logs every open.

## Build
- `src/app/(staff)/_viewer/`: the viewer pane component, built to D03's approved design from U00 components (the box position is the only inline style allowed, rule 1). It takes a list of source pointers (EV-5) and a current index, and shows by pointer kind:
  - document page and box: the stored page image (E00), masked through I00's `redactPageImage` (cached write-once by its key, so a second open costs nothing), with the box drawn over it and scrolled into view, the page number, the document's name, kind and period;
  - sheet, row and column (EV-14): the sheet as a table around the cell, the cell highlighted with words as well as colour, the sheet name and file;
  - QBO line (TB-10): the snapshot's date, the account, and the transaction or trial balance line with its QBO id or "composite key", from B04's stored snapshot;
  - client answer, CRA data capture, last year's return, a reason a person wrote: the stored record with its date and author.
  Next and previous step through the list in order (TB-9's order from `sourcesOf`), with "source 2 of 5".
- `src/app/(staff)/source/[...pointer]/`: the same viewer as a full page, for the second window. Opening from any screen calls `window.open(url, name)` (a fixed window name, no `noopener`) and later clicks re-target that window, so it follows every selection and tab change (rule 20); opening a source adds no history entry (rule 18).
- Every open goes through V00's `canSee` (a pointer on a return the person may not see answers "not found") and writes V00's `logSourceOpen` (SEC-3), including opens in the second window.
- SEC-4: page images are only ever served masked; there is no route that serves an unmasked page image to a screen. Text around a sheet cell or record is masked through U02's `mask.ts`.
- Timing (RV-4, ARC-11): the viewer marks the click and the source paint with `performance.mark`; the stored page, its boxes and the masked image come from E00's stored results and the cache, never by reading the document again.
- Shortcuts through U01: next source, previous source, open in second window, back to the work.
- Focus moves into the viewer when it opens and back to the clicked number when it closes (rule 20).

## Test fixtures (spec-writer)
- From the test world: a C01 return with a statement fact (page and box), a CSV trial balance fact (cell), a QBO account line and transaction (B04 stand-in), a client answer, and a figure built from five sources; the largest test-world return and document available when the spec is written; a page carrying a planted SIN and bank account number.

## Golden files (spec-writer)

## Acceptance checks
1. RV-4, EV-5, EV-14: for the statement fact, the viewer shows the right page with the box drawn over the figure, scrolled into view, and F09's `valueInBox` holds for the shown box; for the sheet fact, the right cell is highlighted with words; for the QBO line, the snapshot date, account and QBO id show.
2. RV-4: for the figure built from five sources, next and previous step through all five in `sourcesOf` order, showing "source n of 5".
3. RV-4, ARC-11 timing (cloud, production build): on the largest test-world return, from click to source paint is under 1000 ms for a page never opened before (the masked image not yet cached), measured by `performance.mark`.
4. SEC-4: the planted SIN and bank account number are blacked out on the served page image (pixels inside their boxes are black) and appear in no text on the page; no route serves the unmasked image (a request to every viewer route for that page returns masked bytes only).
5. SEC-3: each open, in the pane and in the second window, writes one source-log row with who, what, when and screen.
6. SEC-2 through V00: a pointer on a return the preparer may not see answers "not found" and writes no data to the page.
7. RV-4, rule 20: the second window opened with `window.open(url, name)` follows a new click in the main window to the new source; opening a source adds no history entry.
8. RV-53: the viewer matches D03's approved pages within the visual threshold for each pointer kind.
9. RV-54: U02's sweeps pass on the viewer routes; focus moves into the viewer and back; every action works by keyboard alone; the golden ARIA snapshots match.
