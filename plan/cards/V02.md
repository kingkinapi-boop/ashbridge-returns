## When it starts
This card waits until D02 (the CPA review) is approved by Zo at a design sitting (RV-53); the Lead marks D02 done then.

## Goal
The first thing the CPA sees on a return in review: the return in six numbers against last year, the tier and why, the pinned flags red first with their dollar effect and the preparer's answer, what changed since last year, assumptions and client decisions, and the three attestations. Every line is read from code that already decided it; the brief decides nothing and hides nothing (RV-8: the full return always follows, V03).

## Build
- `src/modules/review/brief/` (`briefOf(returnId, deps)`, pure over the contracts passed in; the screen only renders it):
  - Identity (RV-1): corporation, business number, year end, CCPC status and association status (facts with their dots), and the openings (B03's prior closing values that open this year), each with its source pointer.
  - Six numbers (RV-2): T07's `reviewLines` for the latest accepted lock export by meaning (`net_income_tax`, `taxable_income`, `part1_tax`, `ontario_tax`, `instalments`, `balance_or_refund`; `part1_tax` stands for federal tax until the CPA check names its meaning key, `reference/cpa-check.md` item 34, the same row T11 maps), each beside last year's value from B03 and the change in integer cents. A line T07 marks unconfirmed shows "not confirmed in Taxprep yet" beside its value; a missing prior year shows "no prior year" with B03's reason. Never a blank cell.
  - Tier (RV-8): X01's tier, its reasons in words (rule code, plain name, figures read) and the rules version.
  - Pinned flags (RV-2, EX-4): Q00's open flags and exceptions in EX-4 order (red first, then dollar effect), each with its dollar amount and tax effect (CK-6), and the preparer's answer from X00 (for a flag or an exception; kind, text or source); an accepted-risk answer is tagged "for you to judge". An open exception is a red flag, never a dot colour (EV-12).
  - What changed since last year: lines new this year, lines gone since last year, and the ten largest changes by absolute amount, by natural key (count 10 is a firm parameter in the module, named as such).
  - Assumptions and client decisions: every fact feeding a figure whose source is a client answer, and every tax choice typed in Taxprep with its cite (T12), each with its source pointer.
  - Attestations, worked out now, never read from a stored claim: zero orphans (T12 `blockers` holds no orphan, override or dropped cell), diagnostics cleared (T05 `blockingDiagnostics` empty), preparer signed (a preparer version for the latest lock export, and the sign from respond). A failed attestation shows red with what remains, never as passed. Each Warning diagnostic shows with its named preparer's written reason (T05); a Warning with none turns the diagnostics attestation red.
- `src/app/(staff)/review/brief/`: the Return brief tab, shown to every role V00's `canSee` lets see the return (the same record tabs for everyone: decision 0020 Z20-6, A387): the CPA and the owner act on it; the assigned preparer sees it read-only, with no mark, judge, approve or comment control; the brief shows no comments (V04 owns them and keeps the CPA's comments hidden from everyone but the CPA and the owner until `sendForRework`; a draft is not sent); every source opened from it goes through `logSourceOpen` (SEC-3). Who sees a return is V00's rule, unchanged here. Built to D02's approved pages: numeric table for the six numbers, tier tag with words and a "Why this tier" details, pinned flags table, summary lists for the rest, and one link onward to the first section of the full return (V03). One figure prints the same string here as in the sections and the trace (rule 5).
- Shortcuts through U01: next flag, start the full return.

## Spec
- Fixtures: C01 in review with a red and an amber flag, one accepted-risk answer, a tax choice cited by a written reason, a client-answer fact, an unconfirmed review line; C05 with no prior year; a return with a planted orphan left in its latest trace; a Warning diagnostic with its written reason and one without; users: the CPA, the owner, the assigned preparer, preparer B, ops.
- Classes: RV-2 (each of the six numbers equals `reviewLines` and B03, change exact in cents, property over values with a fixed seed); RV-2 attestations (a planted orphan, a planted blocking diagnostic and a missing preparer version each turn their attestation red naming what remains); RV-8 (tier and reasons equal X01's for every test-world kind, golden file); EX-4 and EV-12 (flags red first then dollar effect; an exception never colours a dot); RV-1 (the brief comes first and links on to the first section); RV-1 identity (each item equals its fact or B03 value; a missing one shows 'not known' with no blank cell); RT-17 (each Warning shows its named preparer's written reason; a Warning with none turns the diagnostics attestation red); What changed (property, fixed seed: new and gone lines exactly; the listed ten are the ten largest absolute changes, ties by natural key; fewer than ten lists all; the count is read from the parameter); Assumptions (exactly the client-answer facts feeding a figure and the cited tax choices, none missing, none extra); SEC-2, written from V00's access table (each role and assignment `canSee` allows sees the brief: the CPA and the owner on every return, the assigned preparer read-only with no mark, judge, approve or comment control in the page or its server actions; preparer B and ops, whom V00 does not let see a return in review, get "not found", the same as an address that does not exist); SEC-3 (a source opened from the brief by any role writes one source-log row); RV-53 (matches D02's brief pages within the visual threshold; D02's budget for "read the brief" holds); RV-54 (U02's sweeps; keyboard alone; golden ARIA snapshot of C01's brief in `e2e/staff/__golden__/`).
