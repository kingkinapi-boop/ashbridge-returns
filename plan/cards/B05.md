## Goal
Turn the QBO snapshots B04 stores into the return's books: one Account record per trial balance line, each pointing to its snapshot line, and the adjusting entries, each explained only when it nets to zero and has a type, a reason and a source. A trial balance that does not balance, or whose opening retained earnings do not follow from last year's closing, is refused with the reason. For any account the trace can show its QBO balance, the transactions behind it and each adjusting entry with its reason. Nothing is kept or edited here that belongs in QBO.

## Build
- `src/contracts/books.ts`: the books service interface and result types (Account, Adjusting entry with its explained state, the books result with refusals and flags, the account trace), taken as a parameter by other modules (F10's rule).
- `src/modules/books/read/` (B04's adapter and snapshots, the ledger and the clock come in through contracts):
  - `readReturnBooks(returnId)`: calls B04's `readBooks` for the return's company and year, then writes, for the year-end trial balance snapshot, one Account record per line (account, number, name, type, year-end balance in integer cents with debit positive, the snapshot pointer). TB-1: the trial balance is never edited here; a later read writes new Account versions pointing to the new snapshot.
  - TB-5 refusals: the trial balance does not balance (debits minus credits not zero, with the difference in cents); the company's retained earnings account cannot be found by its QBO sub-type, or there is more than one; opening retained earnings (the year-end trial balance's retained earnings line) differ from last year's closing, computed from the opening snapshot as its retained earnings plus that year's net income from its income and expense lines. When the opening snapshot is empty and the company is in its first year (from the bridge's incorporation date, passed in), opening retained earnings must be zero; when it is empty otherwise, the result says "opening not checked: no evidence" (B03 adds last year's filed return as a second source). A refused books result names each reason; the return's books are not used for figures, and the checks on the Taxprep export (CK-10, CK-11) still run later.
  - Adjusting entries (TB-2): every QBO journal entry in the year whose memo starts with `AJE` or whose Adjustment flag is true is an adjusting entry. Its memo is parsed by the convention `AJE <type>: <reason> | source: <pointer>[; <pointer>]` (types: reclass, accrual, allocation, estimate, correction; pointers: a document fingerprint and page, an onboarding answer key, a CRA capture, last year's return, or a QBO attachment on the entry). It counts as explained only when its lines net to zero in cents, the type is one of the five, the reason is not empty, and at least one source resolves (a written reason alone is not a source). Otherwise it is stored "unexplained" with exactly what is missing, and flagged for a person. Returns never changes the entry; the preparer fixes it in QBO and the books are read again.
  - Other journal entries in the year (no `AJE` memo, no Adjustment flag) are ordinary bookkeeping, but each one dated in the last month of the year or after it is flagged "journal entry not marked as adjusting" for a person to confirm.
  - A transaction or line whose pointer is a composite key (B04's `idKind: "composite"`) is flagged "no QBO id" on the books result.
  - `accountTrace(returnId, accountId)` (TB-7, TB-9): the account's year-end balance with its snapshot pointer, the opening balance, every transaction feeding it in date order with its pointer, every adjusting entry touching it with its type, reason, sources and explained state, and the check that opening plus transactions plus adjusting entries equals the year-end balance in cents (a difference is shown, never hidden). Dots are not set here (L01 sets them where the trace is drawn).
  - Every write carries the snapshot ids and this card's code release as its version stamp (ARC-10) and goes through one transaction.
- `db/schema/30_books.sql` (F01 wrote the tables): add the explained state and its missing parts on adjusting entries, the books result with refusals and flags, and indexes the trace needs; keep F01's append-only rules.
- `src/pipeline/steps/books.ts`: replaces F10's placeholder with `readReturnBooks`, reporting accepted or refused with the reasons, the counts of accounts and adjusting entries, and the flags.
- `src/modules/books/index.ts`: the public exports (B01, B03 and B07 add theirs).

## Acceptance checks
1. TB-1: C01's books give one Account per year-end trial balance line, equal to the golden file, each balance equal to the answer key's adjusted trial balance in cents; a second read writes new versions pointing to the new snapshot and leaves the old ones unchanged.
2. EV-5: every Account, adjusting entry line and trace row carries exactly one QBO pointer naming its snapshot and account (and the transaction id for a transaction).
3. TB-5: the copy one cent off is refused with "trial balance does not balance" and the 1-cent difference; the copy with opening retained earnings $100.00 off is refused naming both amounts; C09 (first year) passes with opening retained earnings zero; a company with no opening snapshot that is not in its first year says "opening not checked: no evidence".
4. TB-2: every C01 and C02 adjusting entry is explained; the entries with no source, an unknown type and an empty reason are each stored unexplained naming exactly what is missing and flagged for a person; property (fast-check, fixed seed): an entry is explained only if its lines net to zero in cents, and any entry whose lines do not net to zero is never explained.
5. TB-2: the ordinary journal entry dated 31 Dec is flagged "journal entry not marked as adjusting"; one dated 15 Mar is not flagged.
6. TB-7, TB-9: `accountTrace` for C01's account 1300 equals its golden file: every transaction and adjusting entry feeding the account in order, each with its pointer and reason, and opening plus activity plus entries equals the year-end balance in cents.
7. ARC-13 property (fast-check, fixed seed): for any generated trial balance in cents, the books are refused exactly when debits minus credits is not zero, and the reported difference equals it.
8. The composite-key transaction is flagged "no QBO id"; a clean C01 read has no such flag (no false alarm).
9. The books step for C01 through the pipeline returns accepted with the counts; for the refused copy it returns refused with the reasons, and later steps see the refusal.
