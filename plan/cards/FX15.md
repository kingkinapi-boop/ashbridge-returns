## Goal
F06's `complete` and `fail` check the job's status only, never the lease holder, so a stale handler (AI or not) can finish or fail a job another worker re-claimed after lease expiry (A446).

## Spec
- `complete` and `fail` take the lease holder and refuse a write from a worker that no longer holds the lease, naming the job id; the job stays as the current holder left it. Planted: worker A claims, its lease expires, worker B claims, A completes: refused; B completes: done.
- A property test over random claim, expire, complete and fail orders on two workers: no job is ever finished by a worker that did not hold its lease at that moment.
