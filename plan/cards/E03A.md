## Goal
The question-bank cards G12 to G17 each need at least one client-askable fact (origin onboarding or qa) per topic, and a fact belongs to exactly one bank item. The G12 spec found none for assets and CCA: R27 (an asset bought but not yet available for use) and R28 (an asset sold or written off in the year) have no askable fact. Before G12 to G17 are specced, the catalogue gains the askable facts their rules read.
