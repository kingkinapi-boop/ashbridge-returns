## Goal
One way for every owner-manager topic to run: code picks the facts, I00 masks them, the AI looks for the issue, code checks every citation, and each kept finding becomes a flag for a person with its facts and a dollar amount code worked out. What code already flags is not repeated. A topic card (I10 to I19) then writes only a prompt, its fact selection and its recordings.

## Build
- `src/modules/ai/checklist/_core/subjects.ts`: AI-2's subjects as a closed list (personal expenses, meals, vehicles, home office, GIFI 9270 Other expenses, shareholder-loan signs code does not catch, CCPC status, association, dividend refunds), and the code-owned subjects AI-2 names with their checks (shareholder-loan continuity CK-43, the 15(2.6) date CK-30, slips CK-38, personal services business signs CK-35, the capital dividend account CK-41, next year's instalments CK-46).
- `src/modules/ai/checklist/_core/topic.ts`: `defineTopic({ id, subject, promptVersion, prompt, modelId, selectFacts, coveringChecks, severity })`. Refuses a subject not on the list, a code-owned subject (naming its check), a blank prompt version or a duplicate id. `selectFacts(returnId, ledger)` gets L00's read-only contract and returns the topic's fact ids, never the whole ledger.
- `src/modules/ai/checklist/_core/job.ts`: builds the `finding` job for a topic and return through I00's `groundedStep` (redaction first, F04 schema, citation checks), and exports the `ai:checklist` job handler for F10 to register (jobs run through A04 and come back later).
- `src/modules/ai/checklist/_core/toFlags.ts`: turns a topic's kept output into flags for a person (CK-42, CK-5): an `issue` finding becomes a flag with its summary, its cited facts and an amount equal to the sum of the cited facts' amounts in cents, read by code (no money fact cited: no amount). A `missing` finding (AI-5) becomes a flag naming where the item should be. A `cannot_tell` answer becomes a flag "AI could not tell" with its reason and no amount (AI-6). A finding whose cited facts are all already cited by a raised flag of one of the topic's covering checks is dropped and counted "already flagged by CK-NN" (AI-2).
- `src/modules/ai/checklist/_core/check.ts`: `topicCheck(topic)` returns an engine check in Q00's shape: kind `ai`, the topic's severity, its input the topic's stored kept output for the return (none yet: Q00 gives "not checked: no evidence"), its rule `toFlags`. Each topic card adds its check to the pipeline's list.
- AI-7: the module gets read-only ledger and documents contracts (a type test); no function returns pass or fail, or writes a fact status, an answer, an exception status or an approval; the flag type has no status field.
- Tests use recorded answers only (A04's `recorded` engine). A fixture topic with its recordings lives in `__fixtures__/`, not in the product.
- `// @mutate` on `topic.ts` and `toFlags.ts`; mutation 100 per marked file (ARC-15).

## Acceptance checks
1. AI-2: the fixture topic on made-up C01 produces one kept finding citing two facts, stored as one flag with those facts, the summary and the stamp (model, prompt version, OCR engine, mapping release; AI-10).
2. AI-2: `defineTopic` refuses a subject off the list and each of the six code-owned subjects, naming its check; a finding citing only facts a raised CK-43 flag already cites is dropped and counted "already flagged by CK-43"; one that also cites a new fact is kept.
3. AI-2 with AI-9: the job sent holds only the facts `selectFacts` chose (a planted unrelated fact is absent) and a planted SIN fact arrives masked.
4. CK-42: for each of personal expenses, meals, vehicles and home office, a fixture topic's flag carries its facts and amount; property (fast-check, fixed seed): the amount equals the sum of the cited facts' cents for any set of cited facts.
5. AI-6: a recorded `cannot_tell` answer becomes a flag for a person with its reason, never a pass and never a value.
6. AI-4 through I00: a finding with a citation to a fact id that does not exist is dropped and counted against the topic, and never becomes a flag.
7. AI-7: a sweep of the module's exports finds no function returning pass or fail or writing a status, answer or approval; a type test shows the deps have no write method; no file imports a ledger write function.
8. CK-2 through Q00: a topic with no stored output gives "not checked: no evidence", not a pass.
