## Spec (a spec worker who has not touched A07 or A07B)

2. Every ExcelJS value shape (number, boolean, date, formula, error, richText, and a hyperlink wrapping each text kind) reads with its true type and text; a hyperlinked number, boolean or formula cell keeps its type and its formula.
