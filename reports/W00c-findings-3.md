Every written total, `rolls`, `exportActivity`, `rowsMissingFromExport`, `fiscalYear.days` and adjusting-entry `amount` (28 of 28) already agrees with its computed twin. *Where else:* src/contracts/records.ts:37 (`stampRecord`, a z.record keyed by NonBlank, drops `__proto__`); bridge.ts:115 (safe only while HANDOFF_ID_PATTERN refuses it); gaps/bank/index.ts:111 and W00b's guard.ts:108 (JSON.parse with no repeated or reserved key scan); SC2's R75 (comparing `rowsInExport` with CSV lines would fail 9 accounts); B04 and JH0 if they read `exportRows` as a transaction count.

**RC-C. Only references someone listed get resolved** (finding 6).
