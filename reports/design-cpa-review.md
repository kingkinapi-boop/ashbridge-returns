# Design: the CPA review and its source viewer (3 versions)

Branch `claude/design-cpa-review`. Brief: `design/briefs/cpa-review.md`. Open `design/prototypes/cpa-review/index.html` in a browser (needs internet for the CSS and JS from cdn.jsdelivr.net: GOV.UK Frontend 6.5.1, MOJ Frontend 11). Made-up data only: Maple Ridge Consulting Inc. (Test), red tier, and Queen West Design Studio Inc. (Test), green tier, both from `reference/sample-clients/` answer keys. Prototype date pinned at 10 Mar 2026. Last year's income figures are made up (the samples hold only opening balances); balance sheet "last year" is the real opening balance.

Pages are generated by `node design/prototypes/cpa-review/_build/build.mjs` (no packages); `_build/check.mjs` lints links, h1, inline style, class prefixes and em dashes (0 issues on 221 pages). Shared files: `assets/ashbridge.css` (Ashbridge palette as GOV.UK functional colours plus the `app-` classes), `assets/review.js` (select, step, keys, second window).

## Reviewed mark (RV-5 as changed, end state v1.1 item 6)
Five sections in fixed order: Flags first, Balance sheet, Income statement, Schedule 1, Other schedules. Each carries an explicit "Reviewed" mark with who and when (key `r`). The mark comes off with the reason shown ("Why the mark came off", before and after values, who, when) when a number in it changes: see `red-rework-*` pages. Flags can be marked only when none is open (the button is absent and the page says how many are left). Approve is absent, not disabled, until all five are marked; the page lists what is left as links. Approve appears on `green-ready-*` pages and leads to `green-ready-approved.html`.

## States covered (use "Prototype: jump to a state" at the foot of any page)
Normal and flagged (red return), green return, in progress, every section marked, back from rework (two changed numbers, Income statement mark off, Changes tab), empty (queue empty, comments empty on green), error (comment form with the GOV.UK error summary), loading source, failed source, number with no evidence ("Not checked: no evidence", CK-2), non-document sources as cards (entry, client answer, CRA capture, computed sum), a spreadsheet source (sheet, row, column), approved, sent back.

## Budgets as designed
Open a return: 1 load, 1 click. Source of any number: 0 loads, 1 click or `j`/`k` then it shows at once (all three panes in version 1). Next flag, number, section, mark: 1 key. Comment: `c` then submit, 2 fields. Approve: 1 click when every section is marked. Brief fits one laptop screen at 1440 by 900 (flags list scrolls inside).
