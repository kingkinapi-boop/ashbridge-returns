## What is drawn
- **Trace (D08).** The six classes in words (traced, rolled forward, calculated, orphan, overridden, dropped), with collapsed groups and their counts: traced 12, rolled forward 3, calculated 8, allowed typing 3, imported from CRA 3, the one rounding line, and (second return) cells linked from another return. An edited CRA cell is an override with both values. "Changed outside the trace" is its own flagged row. Whole dollars only. Orphans open the cite step (a source pointer or a written reason, exactly one); candidates list "Holds this exact value" first and "Rounds to this value" apart, none preselected. Tax choices carry a kind tag (CCA claim, dividend designation, election, business limit share, loss or donation claim).
- **Exceptions (D08).** One list, red first then dollar effect. Fixed, Explained, Accepted risk, none preselected. "Per client" alone is refused. "Use last year's answer" only on a click. Accepted risk reads "for the CPA to judge" and the preparer's page has no judge control. The new flags (CK-38 widened, CK-51 to CK-59, AI-13) are plain rows in this one list: slips implied, input tax credit before registration, pay to a relative, small supplier limit, late-filing exposure, prepaid expense, quick method, grant in suspense. No new screen.
- **CPA comments (D12).** One h2 per RV-1 section. A drafted fix, tagged "AI draft", for simple comments only: a fact value (before and after) or a Taxprep entry (becomes a to-do). Approve draft and Reject draft for the assigned preparer only; Reject needs a reason. "Cannot tell" shows its reason, with no approve.
- **Two returns.** Maple Ridge Consulting Inc. (Test): 9 open trace rows of 38 cells, 13 exceptions (5 red, 8 amber), 6 comments in 5 sections. Eglinton Retail Ltd. (Test): linked-from-another-return cells, an edit to a prior-year cell, 5 exceptions, no comments.
- **States:** normal, flagged, error (`#/trace/error`, `#/exceptions/error`, `#/comments/error`), empty (`#/trace/empty`, `#/exceptions/empty`, comments none yet), held by someone else (`?hold=other`), and the four stages (import, upload, traced, rework).

## Judgment calls, for the Lead to log as amber if kept
1. **At 1093 x 525 the pane is 400 px wide, not 340** (the list column gives the room; the steps column is 150 px). Without it the pinned foot of an exception left the source box under 50 px. Only version B3b's CSS changes; at 1280 px and wider the pane is B's 420.
2. **Compact pane items** for these three steps: Prev and Next sit beside the state line, the secondary detail (flag, dollar effect, what the answers mean; section, type and the change; more about the cell) is one collapsed GOV.UK details under the source, the cite form's own legend is for screen readers only (the two group titles name the choices), a label and its box share a row in the foot, and the source caption keeps one line when the screen is under 580 px high. The full caption stays in the second window.
3. **"Cannot tell" reasons are one short line** ("the file holds no loan rate or start date."). The placement is under the state line so it is read without a click.
4. **Flags have no new screen**: they are made-up rows (13 on Maple Ridge, 5 on Eglinton). Clause numbers are not printed on the page.
5. **Approve draft** is a real button but only changes the page's state; "A" and "C" move focus to Approve draft and to the answer box and never act (rule 22).
6. Sources for the shared viewer are in this folder's `assets/sources3b.js` (made-up); `sources.js` is unchanged.
